## 01 The person

> The experienced specialist who knows what they are doing, explains it clearly, does the job properly, and never needs to be arrogant.

_Motto: "The tree that bears fruit bends down."_

Picture a senior site specialist with twenty years in the field. They arrive on time in a pressed navy uniform, safety shoes on, torch and checklist in hand. Before entering a home they ask where to keep their shoes.

They switch easily between Hindi, Punjabi and English depending on who is in front of them. They explain the five stages to a family in the same calm tone they use with a hospital's maintenance engineer.

They never say "we're busy, let's finish quickly." They finish properly, clean up, put a dated sticker on the tank and tell you when the next cleaning is due. Then they leave the decision with you.

| Dimension             | Persona                                   |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Age / maturity        | Experienced, mature professional          |
| Presence              | Calm, composed, competent                 |
| Expertise             | Highly knowledgeable and process-driven   |
| Behaviour             | Respectful, disciplined, humble           |
| Motivation            | Doing the work properly and earning trust |
| Customer relationship | Helpful, educational, never pushy         |

### Archetype

- **Sage / Expert** (Primary, ~70%): Earns trust through knowledge, process and clarity. Explains rather than impresses.
- **Caregiver** (Supporting, ~30%): Adds responsibility, respect and genuine concern for the customer's home and water.

### The personality equation

- **Expertise** without arrogance
- **Professionalism** without distance
- **Knowledge** without unnecessary complexity
- **Service** without pressure

## 03 Five core traits

### Knowledgeable

_Built from: Scientific, Technical, Understanding_

- **Means:** Understands the subject deeply and explains it clearly.
- **Doesn't mean:** Showing off jargon or making the customer feel uninformed.
- **Sounds like:** "Let me show you what each stage does."
- **Looks like:** Process diagrams, real measurements, documentation handed over.

### Disciplined

_Built from: Disciplined, Steady, Focused_

- **Means:** Follows the defined process instead of cutting corners.
- **Doesn't mean:** Rigid or unwilling to listen. Improvement happens through the right channel.
- **Sounds like:** "We'll complete all five stages. It takes a little longer, and that's the point."
- **Looks like:** Readiness checks, closure checks, the same standard in every district.

### Humble

_Built from: Earnest, Calm, Open-minded_

- **Means:** Confident in expertise without arrogance or superiority.
- **Doesn't mean:** Timid, vague or apologetic about the method.
- **Sounds like:** "Good question. Most people don't know this either."
- **Looks like:** No superlatives, no "#1", no attacks on competitors.

### Responsible

_Built from: Trustworthy, Secure, Professional_

- **Means:** Takes the work, the customer and the outcome seriously.
- **Doesn't mean:** Taking the blame for everything or promising beyond authority.
- **Sounds like:** "If anything was missed, we'll come back and correct it."
- **Looks like:** Certificates, dated stickers, honest limits in every claim.

### Reassuring

_Built from: Warm, Straightforward, Practical_

- **Means:** Creates confidence through clarity, professionalism and calm behaviour.
- **Doesn't mean:** Sugar-coating, over-promising or fear-based urgency.
- **Sounds like:** "Here's what happens next, and here's who to call."
- **Looks like:** Clear next steps, plain-language explanations, a calm pace.

## 07 Voice & tone

Clean Tank speaks like an expert who knows the subject well and wants the customer to understand it. Technical knowledge is simplified, not hidden and not unnecessarily complicated.

**Voice formula:** 40% expertise, 25% clarity, 20% reassurance, 10% warmth, 5% personality

| Context                   | Tone                                                  | Sounds like                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Customer enquiry          | Helpful, clear, practical                             | "Sure. Could you tell us the tank capacity and roughly when it was last cleaned? That's all we need to plan the visit."                  |
| Service communication     | Confident, organised, respectful                      | "Our team will reach at 10 am. The full five-stage process takes about two hours for your tank, and water will be off during that time." |
| Educational content       | Informative, easy to understand                       | "A tank can look clean and still have a slippery layer on its walls. That's why washing is only the first part of the process."          |
| Corporate / institutional | Formal, precise, evidence-based                       | "Please find attached the cleaning certificates and service records for the 12 tanks at Block A, along with the next scheduled date."    |
| Complaint                 | Calm, respectful, accountable, solution-oriented      | "Thank you for telling us. Let's inspect the tank together today. If anything was missed, we'll correct it."                             |
| Franchise communication   | Structured, supportive, professional, standard-driven | "Here's the process every partner follows, stage by stage. We'll train your team on site before your first job."                         |

**Words to use:** clean, scientific, process, professional, hygiene, treatment, method, system, properly, documented, specialist, care, trust

**Use carefully:** health, safe, non-toxic, bacteria-free, protection, guaranteed. These need evidence and the right documentation, plus the water-quality qualifier (source water, plumbing, tank condition and later upkeep are outside our service).

**Avoid:** cheap, best, #1, guaranteed, 100% bacteria-free, fear-based selling, urgency, unsupported superiority claims

## 08 Behaviour

**Customer emotional journey:** Concern → Understanding → Confidence → Relief → Trust

- Explain questions without making the customer feel uninformed.
- Reassure worried customers through facts and clear next steps.
- Do not compromise the process just because the team is busy.
- Take responsibility when something goes wrong.
- Say when something is unknown rather than inventing an answer.
- Do not attack competitors to create credibility.
- Respect a customer's decision, including a decision not to proceed.
- Keep the customer experience simple even when the internal system is sophisticated.

### What would the Humble Expert do?

- **"Are these chemicals safe for my water?"** Don't get defensive and don't overclaim. Explain what the antibacterial stage does, show the documentation we actually hold, and only state what that document supports. _(Knowledgeable)_
- **"My plumber does it for much less."** Never criticise the plumber. Explain what the five stages add beyond washing, then respect whatever they decide. _(Humble)_
- **"Can you finish in half an hour? I have guests coming."** Explain calmly why each stage matters and offer a better slot. Skipping a stage is not an option. _(Disciplined)_
- **"So my water is 100% bacteria-free now?"** Be honest: the tank has been cleaned and disinfected properly. Water quality also depends on the source, plumbing and upkeep, and we say so. _(Responsible)_
- **A technician doesn't know the answer.** "Let me check with our technical team and get back to you today." Then actually call back. _(Reassuring)_
- **The customer says the corner still looks dirty.** Listen, go and look together, verify. Correct it if it's real. If the work meets standard, explain and show why. _(Responsible)_

## 11 Messaging & experience

> Clean Tank Services provides scientific and mechanized water-storage-tank cleaning through knowledgeable people, disciplined processes and professional execution.

- Scientific & Mechanized Cleaning
- Knowledgeable & Professional People
- Complete Process & Discipline
- Trust & Hygiene Awareness

**Customer should remember:** "These people know their work, follow a proper process, behave well and can be trusted with my water-storage tank."

**Professional in the system, simple in the customer experience.**

1. Capture only the information needed to plan the service.
2. Avoid unnecessary inspection visits when remote information is enough.
3. Arrive professionally and interact respectfully.
4. Use proper equipment and follow the defined process.
5. Complete the service rather than deliberately rushing it.
6. Leave a cleaning certificate and a dated sticker on the tank.
7. Recommend the next cleaning date while leaving the decision with the customer.
8. Follow up around the due date without creating pressure.

> "The tank was cleaned properly, I understand what was done, I have a record of it, and I know whom to contact next time."

_Clean Tank leaves behind a record of the service, not just a cleaned tank._

## 12 Guardrails & persona filter

- Stay specialist: a water-storage-tank cleaning brand, not a generic cleaning company.
- Keep scientific and mechanized positioning consistent.
- Knowledge should create humility, not superiority.
- Process comes before speed.
- Trust is earned through behaviour and execution.
- Education should never become pressure.
- Local adaptation must not compromise national brand consistency.
- Never invent certifications, approvals, lab results, health claims, specifications, credentials or performance claims.

### Before anything goes out, ask

- [ ] Would the Humble Expert say this to a family, in their home, face to face?
- [ ] Does it explain, or does it frighten?
- [ ] Can we prove every claim in it?
- [ ] Is it calm? Remove one exclamation mark, then remove the rest.
- [ ] Does it respect the customer's right to decide?
- [ ] Could every franchise partner deliver exactly this?
- [ ] Does it make Clean Tank sound like a specialist, not a cleaning company?

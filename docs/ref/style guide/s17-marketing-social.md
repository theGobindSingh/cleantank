# Clean Tank Services: Style Guide
# 17. Marketing & Social

> This pillar governs how the Clean Tank Services identity stretches across feeds, stories, reels, thumbnails, WhatsApp cards, ads, notice boards, newspaper space and franchise partners' local posts, without breaking recognition. Marketing is where most people first meet the brand and where drift happens fastest: many hands, tight deadlines, many formats. Under the v2 "Quiet precision. One standard, every tank." direction, every social creative is a small spec sheet: a primary title band with a mono series label and a condensed caps headline, one still documentary photo, and a mono footer row. For Clean Tank the stakes are also legal and ethical: this is where a careless "safe" or "best" line, an unconsented face or a borrowed health statistic would undo the Humble Expert in one post. The system is templates and rules, not one-off artwork. Authority lives in the visuals; humility lives in the words.

## 17.1 Principles and inheritance

| v2 principle or brand trait | Visible behaviour in marketing |
|---|---|
| Exact | One template per format, fixed zones, fixed sizes in px. Real measurements and named steps. A post is built from a locked file, never a screenshot |
| Ordered | Title band at top, photo, rule, mono footer, always in that order. Series labels with a running number (`HYGIENE NOTE / 04`). Strict 64 px margin |
| Restrained | One idea per post. White or stone ground, no gradients, no shadows, no badges, no decoration. Accent used once, as a marker. Generous empty space |
| Documented | Photos are documentary stills of real work. Sticker, certificate and dated records lead the Proof pillar. Every claim has evidence on file |
| Honest materials | Concrete, steel, water, equipment, gauges, shown as they are under the muted grade `Clean Tank Daylight v2` (s08). No staged readings, no shock shots |
| Knowledgeable | Education and Process posts lead. Diagrams (s09), named steps, honest limits |
| Humble (words) | No superlatives, no competitor or plumber comparisons. Never "best", "#1", "guaranteed", "100% bacteria-free" |
| Responsible | Every health line carries the water-quality qualifier. Evidence-pending claims never appear on a creative |
| Reassuring (words) | Headline as a calm question or plain statement. No countdowns, no "only today", no urgency badges |
| s01 "People at work" | Technicians shown working, not posing, with written consent (s08) |
| Colour roles | Neutral (white and stone) carries the creative, Primary (navy) is the band, Accent (coral) is one marker. Secondary is TBD and unused here |

Persona filter for every creative: would the Humble Expert say this face to face, does it explain rather than frighten, can every claim be proven, is it calm, does it respect the customer's right to decide, could every franchise partner deliver it?

| Franchise | `NETWORK / NN` | 10% | The Authorised Service Provider network | LinkedIn post, brochure link | No figures inside images. Figures only in caption or brochure beside the full disclaimer (17.9) |

The number is a running issue number inside that series, two digits, never reused. It is never a count of tanks, customers or jobs. A series name is a label, not a claim: any creative that carries a health line still carries the qualifier. A month's plan has one pillar owner and one approver per post. If a week has only two good posts, post two.

## 17.7 Copy rules on visuals

| Rule | Spec |
|---|---|
| Headline form | A calm question or a plain statement, max 8 words, written in sentence case and set in condensed caps by style. "When was your tank last cleaned?" not "Your water could be dangerous!" Caps is a typographic style of the band, never a raised voice |
| Persona rewrites apply | "Book NOW before it's too late!" becomes "If your tank is due for cleaning, we can help you plan the service." "Small investment, high profit!" becomes "A structured service business you can build in your district, with training and support." |
| Punctuation | No exclamation marks on creatives by default; never two or more. No ellipsis suspense |
| Banned words | best, #1, guaranteed, 100% bacteria-free, "kills all germs", "zero risk", "India's leading" (unless evidenced), any attack on plumbers or competitors |
| Health lines | Any "safe", "healthy", "bacteria-free" or hygiene line carries: "Water quality also depends on source water, plumbing, tank condition and later maintenance, which are outside our service." On a creative it uses the P1H or P1HC zone (28 px minimum, Neutral 900, never in the band); if it cannot fit, the line is removed from the image and the claim goes in the caption with the qualifier |
| Evidence-pending claims | Never on any creative until evidenced and approved: Clean Tank Agent properties (non-toxic, eco-friendly, 3-month effect), "60% of diseases" statistic, CPWD DSR inclusion, any WHO, FDA or government approval |
| ISO | Only the ISO line from docs/PRODUCT.md (Claims), filled from `src/site.ts`. Not on stickers (s10). Never "accredited" |
| Numbers | ₹ before number, en-IN grouping (20,00,000 L), dates "03 Oct 2026", non-breaking space before units |
| Contacts | Only the phones and email in `src/site.ts`, the Gobind Galaxy email and the website in INFO-BANK §1. One phone number per creative |
| Emoji | None on creatives. None in captions at launch (open item) |
| Origin story and early dates | Not used until verified (LIC Lucknow vs Kanpur, 2006 to 08). "In business since 2008" and "15+ years" are safe |

## 17.8 Captions, hashtags and bilingual rules

**Caption structure (sentence case, plain):**

1. Line 1: the question or the fact (aim for 125 characters or fewer so it is not cut off, verify).
2. Two or three short lines of explanation.
3. One next-step line, offered not pushed: "If your tank is due, we can help you plan the service."
4. Contact line: phone or WhatsApp, website.
5. Qualifier line when any health line appears (17.7).
6. Hashtags.

**Hashtags:** 3 to 5 at the end of the caption, never in the image, never stacked mid-sentence. Pool: `#CleanTankServices` (brand), `#WaterTankCleaning`, `#TankCleaning`, `#CleanTankHealthyLife` (tagline, optional), plus one local tag only where service exists (Punjab, Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Jharkhand, Maharashtra). No trending tags unrelated to water-tank cleaning. Alternate hashtag sets are logged in the campaign toolkit.

**Bilingual creatives (Hindi, Punjabi, Hinglish):**

| Rule | Spec |
|---|---|
| Layout | Same grid and zones as English (P1I band height is the only change), never a separate design |
| Type | Anek Devanagari or Anek Gurmukhi (s04) 700 for headline, wdth 100, no caps; +10% size; line height 1.65 or 1.60; headline max 8 words. Series label in Anek 600, no mono. Footer stays Latin |
| Terms | "Cleaning Certificate", "Cleaning Date Sticker" and the 5 step names stay identical in every language, spelled as in the approved glossary (open) |
| Hinglish | Natural customer phrasing allowed in captions and WhatsApp (for example "Tank साफ करवाना है"). Headlines on creatives are English, Hindi or Punjabi, not mixed |
| Same claims | Qualifier, disclaimer and contacts translated by a person; never machine-only |
| Review | A native speaker reads every Hindi and Punjabi creative before release (as s11). Name the reviewer in the toolkit |
| Pair | The Hindi and Punjabi version of a post goes out the same day as the English one when the audience warrants it; do not stack three languages on one image |

## 17.17 Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Ask a calm question as the headline, set in the band | Use fear words, exclamation marks or a raised voice |
| Show a dated sticker and certificate as proof | Show a shocking dirty-tank photo |
| Keep one idea per post and leave space | Stack five messages on one image |
| Put the qualifier with health lines | Say "safe" or "bacteria-free" without it |
| Show technicians working, with written consent | Use stock handshakes, posed smiles or unconsented faces |
| Write "depends on your organisation" for frequency | Print a single flat cleaning interval |
| Keep text in the band and the footer | Put text on photos, faces or dials |
| Keep accent to the label and one Level Line | Use accent for fills, buttons, badges or urgency |
| Use square or 4 px corners, 1 px rules | Use shadows, gradients, rounded pills or circles |
| Reply to every review calmly | Delete or fabricate reviews |
| Post festive greetings as plain cards | Use religious or national symbols as ornaments |

## 17.18 Accessibility and India notes

| Topic | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Contrast | All text sits on solid Primary 700, white or stone: pairs in 17.4 (4.5:1 normal, 3:1 large). Accent 500 only as a graphic (Level Line) |
| Alt text | Every image post carries alt text of 125 characters or fewer (s08), in the post language, starting with the subject, no health claims. Never put essential information only in an image: it also appears in the caption |
| Captions on video | Every video is captioned; burned-in plus SRT or VTT in English, Hindi and Punjabi (s11): 5% of short side (54 px), Hindi and Punjabi 5.5% (59 px), white on Primary 900 at 80% |
| Motion | No flashing: three flashes or fewer in one second (WCAG 2.3.1). Reduced motion respected (s11). The Level Line may draw once (s06); on social it is static |
| Text size | 28 px minimum on 1080 canvas; avoid thin weights; condensed caps headlines stay 700 |
| Indic scripts | Anek Devanagari and Anek Gurmukhi, set at +10% size, tested on a mid-range Android phone; native review. Plex Mono has no Indic glyphs, so Indic labels use Anek |
| Low bandwidth | Export JPG at 85% quality, under 1 MB per post, under 150 KB for Google uploaded ads. Flat bands compress well |
| Language | Plain words; short sentences; the same term for the same thing in all languages |

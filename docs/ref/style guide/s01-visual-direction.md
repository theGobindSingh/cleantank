## 01.2 Big idea and principles

**Big idea:** ***Quiet precision. One standard, every tank.***

**Because:** the persona names a calibrated pressure gauge as the brand's tool and a well-run hospital lab as its place. The franchise promise is one standard across districts. The look therefore behaves like a test report: ruled, numbered, exact, and calm. It earns trust by being consistent, not by being decorated.

| # | Principle | Definition | So we | Not |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Exact** | Every value is precise, readable at a glance, and the same every time. | use a strict grid, 1 px rules, mono labels, tabular numerals, exact values (liters, bar, dates). | decorative flourishes, rounded "about" visuals, approximate numbers. |
| 2 | **Ordered** | Structure is visible and the same in every district. | number sections (02.1), tag items with mono reference codes (REF CT-PR-05), keep zones separated by visible rules. | free-form collage, one-off layouts per franchise. |
| 3 | **Restrained** | Few elements, deliberate empty space, one idea per section. | keep at least 60% of any view neutral (white or stone), one key figure per view, functional motion only. | crowded flyers, countdowns, urgency devices, decorative reveals. |
| 4 | **Documented** | We leave behind a record, not only a clean tank. | make stickers, certificates, job cards and checklists look like spec sheets and show them as hero objects. | stock hero photos with no document in sight. |
| 5 | **Honest materials** | Show what the work is made of: concrete, steel, water, equipment. | photograph real tanks, pumps, hoses and gauges, still and composed, with a muted neutral grade. | glossy renders, cartoon mascots, splashes, lifestyle smiles, costume themes. |

## 01.4 The Quiet Utility mix

Quiet Utility is the name of the v2 direction: Utilitarian structure plus Japandi restraint, with a short list of things we leave behind.

| Source style | What we take | What we reject |
|---|---|---|
| Utilitarian | Strict grid, 1 px rules, ruled cells, mono reference labels, section numbers, solid bands, no decoration | Brutalist noise, scanlines, halftone, ASCII gimmicks, aviation red, 0 px corners everywhere |
| Japandi | Deliberate empty space, one idea per section, honest materials (concrete, steel, water), slow and few reveals | Beige and wood palettes, organic blobs, calligraphy fonts, lifestyle smiles |
| Result | Authoritative, not friendly. A spec sheet with room to breathe. | Anything that needs explaining to be understood |

### Colour stance (LOCK, details in s03)

| Role | Color | Share | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neutral | White `#FFFFFF` and light stone `~#F5F4F1`, text graphite `#25282A` | 60% | Lab, space, room to read. Most pages and documents are white or stone. |
| Primary | Navy `#20334B` | 30% | The pressed uniform and the test-report title band. Header, footer, emphasis bands, table headers, buttons. Not whole pages. |
| Accent | Coral `#EE7555` | up to 10% | Marker only: Level Line, active step, reference tags and index numbers, one key figure per view. |
| Secondary | TBD (owner) | n/a | No value assigned; do not invent one. |

Contrast (WCAG 2.x, computed): Primary on White **12.83:1** · Primary on Stone **11.67:1** · Neutral 900 on White **14.83:1** · Neutral 900 on Stone **13.49:1** · White on Primary **12.83:1** · Accent on White **2.87:1** (fails text and 3:1 UI) · Accent on Stone **2.61:1** (fails) · Accent on Primary **4.47:1** (large text and UI only, fails body) · Neutral 900 on Accent **5.16:1** · Accent 600 `#C54826` on White **4.85:1** · Accent 700 `#A33C1F` on Stone **5.92:1**.

Consequences: accent is never body text on white, never a button fill and never a CTA (primary buttons are primary navy fill with white label). Where a reference tag or index number in accent sits on white it uses accent 600; on stone it uses accent 700; on primary bands it is large text or a rule only. Accent never signals warning, error or urgency: state colors in s03 do that, always with icon plus word.

## 01.6 Category audit and the zag

**Status: to be completed with a 10 to 15 screenshot audit.** Collect screens from Indian tank-cleaning providers, local service flyers, adjacent leaders (hospital labs, water-purifier and facility-management brands). The table below is a hypothesis built from common category codes, not a finding. No competitor is named here.

| Common category code (hypothesis) | Observed in audit? | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bright blue water splashes and crowns | To verify | **Break** | Excessive splashes are on the persona avoid list. |
| Droplet icons in the logo | To verify | **Break** | Generic; no ownership. Logo route in s02. |
| Glossy, 3D cartoon tanks | To verify | **Break** | Reads cheap and toy-like. |
| Before/after shock photos | To verify | **Break** | Frightens. Replace with process and record. |
| Red or orange urgency badges | To verify | **Break** | Banned. Pressure tactic. |
| Yellow flyers with rays | To verify | **Break** | Old collateral look; cheap flyer aesthetic. |
| Blue as the water signal | To verify | **Bend** | Keep the blue family but as deep navy in solid bands, never bright or gradient. |
| Large phone number visible | To verify | **Keep** | Customers need it. Set as a ruled field in navy. |
| A person with equipment | To verify | **Bend** | Keep for clarity; shoot documentary, in uniform, working, not posing. |
| Service checklist or steps | To verify | **Keep** | Matches our 5-step process; make it the hero diagram. |

**The zag: authority through structure, not decoration.** The category looks loud, wet and decorated. We look quiet and exact: a ruled grid, a navy band, mono reference codes and one coral marker line on a white or stone ground. Navy keeps us legible as a water-service brand; the structure makes us uncopyable at a glance.

## 01.10 Art direction statement (for photographers, illustrators, AI prompts)

*Clean Tank Services looks like a quiet, well-run test laboratory: ordered, documented and exact. Photograph the work as it is: real technicians in pressed navy uniforms doing real tasks, in daylight, on actual rooftops, basements and institutional sites in India. Keep every frame still, composed and documentary, with generous negative space and a muted neutral grade. Show honest materials: concrete, steel, clear water, pumps, hoses, gauges, checklists, dated stickers and certificates. People are shown working, not posing; no stock smiles. Let white or stone space dominate and keep one coral marker at most. Diagrams are flat, ruled and exact, with square caps. Water is shown clear and still, never as a splash. Never use gradients, shadows, glossy renders, cartoon bacteria, bokeh, sun rays, fear imagery, red or orange badges, or money imagery. Do not invent certifications, lab results or clients in any image or caption.*

(approx 150 words)

**Banned list for prompts:** gradient, shadow, splash, droplet crown, bokeh, rays, gloss, 3D cartoon tank, cartoon bacteria, red badge, gold coin, dirty-tank shock shot, lifestyle smile, halftone, scanlines, ASCII, organic blob.

## 01.11 India and culture notes

## 01.15 Accessibility & India notes

- The floor is stated in Rule 01.6. Quiet means restrained, not faint.
- Meaning is never carried by color alone: pair color with text, icon or position (state, due dates, steps).
- Test every look in Devanagari and Gurmukhi; check line height, matras and conjuncts at small sizes and on cheap print. Condensed uppercase treatment is Latin only.
- Alt text on every moodboard image and example. Do/Don't pairs use words, not only symbols.
- Representation requirement: varied, realistic, respectful people in all photography.
- Motion: functional only; honour reduced-motion settings (details in s11).

# Clean Tank Services: Style Guide
# 16. Physical Brand

> This pillar governs everything Clean Tank Services makes that people wear, drive, walk into or read on site: the technician uniform and ID, vehicle livery, equipment decals, the on-site kit, office signage, camp stalls and the small merchandise range. It matters because the promise is delivered by a person arriving at a gate. The v2 direction is Quiet Utility: physical items read as labelled, numbered, documented equipment, like a well-run lab. A faded van or an improvised decal undoes the website in one glance. It reuses logo (s02), color (s03), shape (s05), type (s04) and icons (s10); it never redefines them. All sizes are in mm and are starting values: **verify with vendor and a signed physical sample before any bulk order.**

## 16.1 Principles / inheritance

Big idea: **Quiet precision. One standard, every tank.** Principles: Exact, Ordered, Restrained, Documented, Honest materials.

| Principle | Physical behaviour |
|---|---|
| Exact | Dimensions, positions and sizes are fixed in a drawing. Equipment decals state the step name and unit, nothing improvised. |
| Ordered | Same uniform, van layout and site kit in Kanpur, Kharar and every franchise district. One approved vendor file per item. Square panels, strict alignment, one idea per surface. |
| Restrained | No superlatives, no "No. 1", no tagline stacks. Large primary areas, few elements, deliberate empty space. |
| Documented | Every item carries a mono reference: technician ID, asset code, vehicle reference. Identification is part of the design. |
| Honest materials | Matte fabric, matte cast vinyl, powder-coated aluminium, board, steel. No gloss, bevel, chrome, shadow or gradient. |
| Safety first | Safety beats brand. PPE follows safety norms, not palette. The site is left cleaner than found. |

| Rule of the pillar | Value |
|---|---|
| Palette | Neutral (white, stone) dominant, Primary navy as bands, Accent coral as a small marker only. Proportion 60 / 30 / 10 (s03). The uniform is the one exception (Primary 85 to 95%, Accent 0 to 3%). |
| Corners | Physical: badge and decals about 1.5 mm, folder or diary 2 mm, van panels and signboards square (or a fixed 4 mm where a sign maker needs it), certificate frame square. Never rounded to a pill. Circles only for literal round objects. |
| Lines | 1 px equivalent rules (0.25 to 0.35 mm printed), square caps, miter joins. No shadows, no gradients. |
| Type | Headings and name tapes: Anek Latin condensed (wdth 78), 700, caps. Labels, IDs, codes, phones: IBM Plex Mono 500 or 600 caps, tracking +0.08 to 0.1em. Body: Anek 400. Indic text: Anek Devanagari or Gurmukhi (Plex Mono has no Indic glyphs). |

Persona anchors: arrives on time, asks where to keep shoes, leaves the site clean. The uniform, shoe covers and site kit make that behaviour visible. Humility stays in the words on the item; authority comes from order and consistency.

## 16.8 Sign sizing and legibility distance

Rule: **minimum character cap height (mm) = viewing distance (m) x 8** (consistent with s02: X 80 mm for 10 m). **Recommended: distance x 12.** Use Primary 700 on white or white on Primary 700 only (12.83:1). Matte, non-glare. This is a design benchmark, not a statement of Indian compliance. For reference, ADA 2010 s703 gives at least 16 mm for up to 1.83 m plus 3.2 mm per extra 0.3 m, and tactile signs 1219 to 1524 mm above floor (verify; not a mandatory Indian rule).

| Sign | Viewing distance | Minimum cap height | Recommended | Size and notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Van side logo | 10 m | 80 mm | 100 mm | s02.11 |
| Van rear phone numbers | 5 m | 40 mm | 60 mm | Plex Mono 600 |
| Fascia board | 15 m | 120 mm | 180 mm | See 16.10 |
| Site A3 sign | 3 m | 24 mm | 36 mm | See 16.9 |
| Site A4 sign | 2 m | 16 mm | 24 mm | Door or gate |
| Stall banner | 5 m | 40 mm | 60 mm | See 16.11 |
| Roll-up standee | 3 m | 24 mm | 36 mm | 800 x 2000 mm (s07) |
| Badge name | 0.6 m | 5 mm | 6 mm | 16.5 |

Signage languages: English, Hindi and Punjabi where the site requires all three, in that order, at **equal visual size** (Anek per script, s04). Minimum Indic text 8 pt print (s04). Do not shrink a second script into a subline. State rules (for example language share on name boards) vary: confirm locally before ordering.

## 16.13 Environmental cleanliness standard

"The site is left cleaner than found." Six site-care checks at every job:

| Step | Standard |
|---|---|
| Arrival | On time; badge visible; shoes off or covered; ask where to keep them |
| Protect | Mat at entry, drop cloth under hoses and equipment; sign placed |
| Work | Sludge and wastewater removed to the place agreed with the customer or site, per operations SOP; no discharge onto roads or neighbours |
| Close | Tools wiped, hoses reeled, floor mopped, mat and cloth removed |
| Record | Sticker applied; certificate handed over |
| Walk-round | Customer checks the area and the tank sticker; technician photographs the clean site for the record, with permission |

Photo of the clean site is for internal records and, with consent, in the certificate pack; never as a public before and after unless s08 rules are met.

## 16.17 Accessibility & India notes

- Contrast on printed signs: Primary 700 on white and white on Primary 700 12.83:1; Primary 700 on stone `#F5F4F1` 11.67:1; Neutral 900 on white 14.83:1; Neutral 900 on stone 13.49:1; white on Primary 900 `#0F1824` 17.85:1. Accent on white 2.87:1 and accent on stone 2.61:1 fail, so never for text on light grounds; accent on Primary 4.47:1 only for large detail or the marker stripe. Accent never signals warning or urgency.
- Never use color alone for roles or routes: add a word, icon or shape.
- Hindi (Devanagari) and Punjabi (Gurmukhi) are set at equal visual size with English, Anek per script, Indic minimum 8 pt, native review before print. Mono codes stay Latin (Plex Mono has no Indic glyphs). Marathi needs separate review.
- Local signboard language rules and vehicle advertising rules vary by state: confirm with authority before ordering.
- Heat and sun: choose breathable twill, avoid black bases, UV-test the accent vinyl, plan rain and winter variants.
- Accessible counters at 750 mm for camps; ground-floor offices preferred; entry and aisle widths per India Harmonised Guidelines (door 900 mm, route 1500 mm; verify current text).
- Customers who prefer Hindi or Punjabi should hear it from the technician; badge name may be shown in that script.

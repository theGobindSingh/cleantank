# Clean Tank Services: Style Guide
# 02. Logo System

> This pillar governs the Clean Tank Services logo kit: the brief for the new mark, the three concept routes, the family of lockups, the geometry behind them, clear space, minimum sizes, colour versions, misuse, digital and Indic versions, files and trademark. Clean Tank has no logo yet (the current artwork is a throwaway placeholder and is retired), so this sheet does two jobs. It is the creative brief a designer works from, and it is the rulebook the finished logo must follow. The mark is authoritative and quiet: an instrument mark, not a friendly badge. Every number below holds whichever route is chosen, because every measure is written in the logo's own unit X.

## 02.2 Logo brief (what it must say)

| Brief item | Statement |
|---|---|
| Role | Specialist in water-storage-tank cleaning only. Not a general cleaning company. |
| Must say | **Authoritative**, **scientific and mechanized** (measured, equipment-led), **Quiet Utility** (ordered, ruled, nothing decorative), **one standard, every tank**. |
| Must not say | Friendly, playful, cheap, urgent, or "approved by". |
| Big idea | **A calibrated gauge**: quiet precision, read at a glance (s01). The mark should feel like the scale plate on a well-made instrument: square-ended strokes, a level mark, corners no larger than 2u. |
| Emotional journey | Concern > Understanding > Confidence > Relief > Trust. The mark's job is the last two: it sits on the certificate and the tank sticker as the sign of a job done to a standard. |
| Personality sliders (s01) | Warm/Cool 3 · Soft/Sharp 4 · Airy/Dense 3 · Quiet/Loud 1 · Human/Technical 4 · Still/Lively 1 · Organic/Geometric 5. So: fully geometric, sharp with 2u-max corners, still, quiet. |
| Audiences | Families (clear, trustworthy), institutions and tenders (formal, precise), franchise partners (reproducible by any local sign maker). |
| Languages | Latin master; Hindi and Punjabi companions (02.14). |
| Colour | Only s03 roles: Primary `#20334B`, Neutral (White `#FFFFFF`, Graphite `#25282A`), and Accent `#EE7555` only as one small marker detail, if any. Accent is never the main shape. Secondary is TBD and is not used in the logo. |

## 02.3 What it must avoid, and reproduction constraints

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| Droplet plus swoosh (or any droplet-as-hero, wave, splash) | Category cliche; persona bans excessive water splashes; not ownable or registrable. |
| Glossy, 3D, bevel, shine, drop shadow, gradient, transparency effects | Persona bans glossy; v2 has no shadows or gradients; fails 1-colour production and 16 px. |
| Round stroke caps, round joins, corners above 2u, pill or circle containers | Soft shapes read as friendly, not authoritative (s05: square caps, miter joins). |
| Cartoon tank, face, smile, mascot | Not the Humble Expert voice; not serious. |
| Bacteria, germs, "dirty vs clean" imagery | Persona ban: explain, never frighten. |
| Sun rays, bokeh, starbursts, badges, shields, laurels | Persona ban; also reads as a claim of approval. |
| Hairlines, tiny type, fine detail | Disappear on embroidery, vinyl and at 16 px. |
| Letters or symbols implying certification (tick-in-seal, "ISO" device) | Hard rule: no invented approvals. |

| Surface the logo must survive | Constraint | Version used |
|---|---|---|
| Van side, one-colour vinyl stencil | Flat shapes, minimum gap 3 mm at 1:1 (verify with sign maker) | One-colour primary or primary |
| Uniform, embroidery | Max 2 thread colours, no detail under 1 mm, text cap height 6 mm or more (verify with digitizer) | Embroidery file (02.16) |
| Favicon 16 px | Symbol reads in a 16 x 16 px grid; stroke at least 2 px | Symbol S-cut |
| WhatsApp, Instagram circle avatar | Symbol fits circle radius 40% of width | Avatar tile (02.15) |
| Rubber stamp on certificate | One colour, stroke 0.4 mm or more (verify with stamp maker) | One-colour primary or black |
| Tank sticker (outdoor, UV) | Solid shapes, no tints | Small-use or symbol |
| Signage, hoarding | Vector only; scales from 30 mm to 3 m | Any vector file |
| Black-and-white fax, photocopy, newspaper | Recognisable without colour | One-colour black |

## 02.4 Three concept routes

All routes use the same constants (set in s05 terms): 16u grid, symbol bounding box 12u wide x 16u tall, stroke 1.5u with **square ends and miter joins**, outer corner radius **2u maximum** (2u = 12.5% of symbol height, equal to 4 px at 32 px, the s05 `sm` token; 0 allowed), inner corners 0, one optional accent marker element, wordmark per 02.6. Counters are rectangular.

| | **Route A: Gauge Tank** | **Route B: CT Gauge Monogram** | **Route C: Level Wordmark** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Idea | A tank in cross-section read like a gauge: the level line is the brand's measurement mark. | "C" built from a rectangular tank outline, "T" built from a measurement tick. | The drawn condensed name leads; one level line is the only graphic. |
| Drawing | Upright rectangular tank seen from the side: body 9u x 13u, outline in Primary, a lid line 7u wide on top. A **level line** 1.5u thick runs wall to wall at 9u above the base (the accent marker, if used). Three short scale ticks (1u thick; 2u, 1.5u, 2u long) sit outside the right wall, 3u apart. All ends square. No fill, no water shape. | A square-cornered "C" drawn as a three-quarter tank outline (open on the right, 1.5u stroke, corners at most 2u). Inside it a "T" whose stem is a plain vertical bar and whose crossbar is a short **tick** (the accent marker, if used) at the level of the C's opening. Reads CT; tank and gauge together. | CLEAN TANK SERVICES in the drawn wordmark (02.6) with a **level line**: a bar 1.5u thick, square ends, running under CLEAN TANK and ending in one short upward tick at the right (like a gauge mark). Symbol-only fallback: a Primary square tile (corners 2u max) with a reversed "CT" and the marker bar. |
| Quiet Utility read | Reads as an instrument plate: orthogonal, ruled, scale ticks. | Reads as a standards-body monogram: ordered, symmetrical, squared. | Reads as a spec-sheet header: condensed caps over a rule. |
| 16 px reading | Outline plus level line at 2u/2px; ticks dropped (S-cut). | Two letters at 2px stems, ticks dropped. | Wordmark unreadable at 16 px; needs the fallback tile. |
| One-colour | Passes: level line stays a solid bar. | Passes. | Passes. |
| Weakness | Tank + line is a familiar idea; must be drawn with care to be ownable. | Monograms are common; CT may be crowded in the trademark register. | Weak symbol; hardest to defend; avatars rely on a tile. |

**Scoring, re-done for v2** (1 to 5; weights total 100; points = score x weight / 5). Authority is now the top criterion. Scale for authority: 5 = reads like a standards-body or instrument mark (ordered, squared, symmetrical); 3 = neutral; 1 = friendly or decorative.

| Criterion | Weight | A | B | C |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reads as authoritative (instrument, standard, institution) | 20 | 4 | 5 | 4 |
| Says "specialist in tanks" at a glance | 15 | 4 | 3 | 3 |
| Survives 16 px and circle crop | 15 | 4 | 4 | 2 |
| Distinctive and registrable | 15 | 4 | 3 | 2 |
| One-colour production (stencil, embroidery, stamp) | 10 | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| Fits Quiet Utility (square ends, ruled, restrained) | 10 | 4 | 4 | 5 |
| Avoids droplet/swoosh cliche | 5 | 5 | 4 | 5 |
| Pairs with Hindi and Punjabi lines | 5 | 4 | 3 | 4 |
| Franchise partners can reproduce it exactly | 5 | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| **Total (of 100)** | | **81** | **80** | **71** |

(Previous v1 scores were 78, 73, 67 under a "calm and human" criterion that is now removed.)

**Recommendation: draw Routes A and B to the same constants and decide between them; Route C is the fallback and its wordmark is used in every route anyway.** A leads by one point, which is inside scoring error. A wins on category clarity and registrability (a tank plus a gauge in one shape, an ownable form for a descriptive name). B wins on authority and one-colour strength, but says less about tanks. Neither is "more correct"; run the s01 three-words test (target words: precise, professional, trustworthy) on both drawn versions with 5 target users, plus the register check (02.19), then choose. **The final pick is an owner decision.** Whichever route wins, sections 02.5 onward apply unchanged.

## 02.5 Construction, unit X and grid

| Item | Value | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Construction grid | **16u** square cells; symbol bounding box 12u wide x 16u tall | LOCK |
| **X (clear-space and layout unit)** | **Cap height of the "T" in CLEAN TANK = 4u = one quarter of symbol height.** In symbol-only files X = symbol height / 4 | LOCK |
| Stroke weight (standard cut) | 1.5u = 0.375X (condensed wordmark stems measure about 0.2X; verify on outlined artwork) | LOCK |
| Stroke weight (S-cut, small) | 2u = 0.5X | LOCK |
| Stroke ends and joins | **Square ends, miter joins** on every stroke and tick (s05.4). No round caps or joins | LOCK |
| Corner radius | **Outer 2u maximum** (equals the s05 `sm` 4 px at a 32 px symbol); designer picks one value from 0 to 2u and uses it on every outer corner. **Inner corners 0** | LOCK |
| Marker element | At most one element (level line or tick), stroke 1.5u (S-cut 2u), at most 12% of symbol ink area. Optional (02.10) | LOCK |
| Detail floor | No gap or stroke under 1u in standard cut, 2u in S-cut | LOCK |
| Symbol ink | Solid shapes and strokes only; no tints, no gradients, no shadows | LOCK |

| Lockup geometry (in X) | Value |
|---|---|
| Symbol height | 4X (width 3X) |
| Gap, symbol to wordmark | 1X |
| CLEAN TANK | Cap height 1X, width about 7.5X (condensed; planning value) |
| Gap between lines | 0.5X |
| SERVICES | Cap height 0.7X, tracked so its width equals CLEAN TANK's width (tracking not above +0.60em; if it would be, raise cap height to 0.8X) |
| Wordmark block position | Vertically centred on the symbol |
| Horizontal lockup total width | About 11.5X (planning value; confirm on drawn artwork; all minimums in 02.9 are derived from it) |

Rationale lines (one per decision, to be completed by the designer): why this tank proportion; why the level line sits at 9u of 16u above the base; why the chosen corner radius (0 to 2u); why the tick spacing is 3u.

## 02.6 Wordmark typography

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Base | **Anek Latin** (s04; SIL OFL 1.1), condensed cut `wdth` 78 (`ct.font.width.condensed`), converted to outlines. Width comes from the real `wdth` axis only: never scaled, never faux-condensed. Artwork never depends on an installed font |
| CLEAN TANK | Weight 700, all caps, tracking start value **+0.04em** (headings use +0.01em; a logo reads better a little looser, designer fixes by eye on outlined art), optical kerning on T-A and A-N pairs checked by eye |
| SERVICES | Weight 700 at wdth 78 (a lighter weight thins out at 0.7X cap height), all caps, cap height 0.7X, tracking set so the line width equals CLEAN TANK (designer fixes; see 02.5 limit) |
| Small-use cut | SERVICES cap height 0.8X, tracking on both lines +0.08em, square terminals kept |
| Custom drawing | A customised drawing of Anek is allowed only for: kerning, one terminal cleanup, and Route C's level line. No new letterforms. Verify OFL reserved-name terms before naming the drawn file after the font |
| No caps typing | The caps wordmark is artwork. Do not retype it live in caps in running text (s04.5) |
| Hindi, Punjabi | Never capitalised, never tracked, always `wdth` 100 (02.14) |

## 02.13 Misuse gallery

| # | Misuse | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stretched, squashed or skewed; or the wordmark condensed by scaling instead of the `wdth` axis | Breaks the 16u proportions and the drawn letter shapes |
| 2 | Recoloured outside s03 roles (all-accent symbol, green or blue "water" version, a Secondary colour) | Accent is the single marker; off-palette breaks recognition |
| 3 | Gradient, shine, 3D, bevel, drop shadow, glow, outline stroke on the wordmark | Persona bans glossy; v2 has no shadows; fails one-colour production |
| 4 | Adding droplet, swoosh, wave, rays, bacteria or a smile | Category cliches and fear imagery |
| 5 | Retyped wordmark in another font, lowercase, or live caps text instead of artwork | Not the registered shape; breaks s04 |
| 6 | Rearranged lockup: moving SERVICES, tilting, rotating, centring, moving the level line | One approved arrangement per version |
| 7 | Low-contrast or mid-tone ground, busy photo, accent ground with white logo (2.87:1) | Unreadable |
| 8 | Crowding the clear space or boxing the logo in a badge, shield, seal, circle or pill | Looks like an approval claim; loses order |

## 02.19 Accessibility, India and trademark

| Topic | Note |
|---|---|
| WCAG | Logos are exempt from contrast rules, but a logo used as the home link is a UI component: ship a high-contrast one-colour version (Primary on White 12.83:1; White on Primary 12.83:1) |
| Alt text | Home-link logo: "Clean Tank Services, home". A repeat logo that is decorative: empty alt |
| Colour independence | One-colour versions stay recognisable; the mark never depends on the accent marker |
| Focus and size | Logo links need a visible focus ring (2 px Primary, 2 px offset, radius 4; white ring on Primary bands, s05) and a target of 44 x 44 px or more (verify s13) |
| Animation | Pausable, never flashing; reduced-motion shows the static file (s11) |
| India | Black-and-white printed invoices, photocopied tender papers and fax-quality stamps are common: the 1c-black version is mandatory in the kit |
| Scripts | Hindi and Punjabi lines from Anek only (wdth 100); native review before first print |

# Clean Tank Services: Style Guide
# 08. Photography & Art Direction

> This pillar governs every photograph that carries the Clean Tank Services name: what is shown, how it is lit, framed, graded and cropped, how it sits under type, and what paperwork sits behind it. Under the v2 "Quiet precision. One standard, every tank." direction, a Clean Tank photograph is still, composed and documentary: honest materials (concrete, steel, water, equipment, gauges), a muted neutral grade, generous empty space, and people shown working, not posing. The photo is the proof; the layout around it is a spec sheet. A stock handshake, a grinning pose or a shock shot of a dirty tank undoes the brand in one frame. Franchise partners will shoot their own jobs, so consent, privacy and honest before/after use are written down here.

Sheet CT-SG-08 | v2.0 | 08 Oct 2026 (Quiet Utility revision) | Tier legend: LOCK = central brand owner approves any change · GUARD = varies inside stated limits · FLEX = judgment within principles

Not covered: illustration, 3D and AI imagery (s09), video (s11), logo rules (s02), social templates (s17), uniform and van (s16). Ratios are WCAG 2.2, computed with Python. All lighting, grade and crop numbers are starting values to confirm on the pilot shoot, not published standards. The v1 grade ("Clean Tank Daylight v1") is retired: do not build from it.

## 08.1 Principles and inheritance

| s01 principle | Photo behaviour | Never |
|---|---|---|
| 5 Honest materials | Concrete, steel, water, hoses, pumps, gauges, shown as they are, still and composed | glossy gear, splashes, glow, renders |
| 4 Documented | A gauge, checklist, sticker or certificate in at least 1 of every 3 images | hero photo with no record |
| 3 Restrained | Eye level, daylight, 40% or more negative space (55% or more on heroes), one subject, no effects | low angles, tilted frames, bokeh, filters |
| 1 Exact | One grade, true gauge readings, legible dates, straight verticals | per-image taste edits, staged readings |
| 2 Ordered | Subject on the grid, aligned to hero columns, tidy site | cluttered, crowded frames |
| Persona: explain, never frighten | Neutral faces absorbed in the task; finished-work moments | dirty-tank shock, worried families, alarm colours |

Five principles for photographers: (1) **Real work, real people**: our own technicians and sites; never stock or AI people. (2) **Honest materials**: concrete, steel, water and equipment, with a person or hands in every equipment frame. (3) **Still and composed**: eye level, daylight, level horizon, empty space left on purpose. (4) **Show the record**: sticker, certificate, checklist, gauge as hero objects. (5) **People working, not posing**: nobody looks at the lens, nobody performs a smile, nobody stands arms-crossed; explain, never frighten.

## 08.3 People, casting and staged vs candid

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Technicians | Real employees and Authorised Service Providers only, never hired actors presented as staff |
| Behaviour | Shown working: eyes on the task, hands busy, mid-step. No look to camera, no thumbs-up, no arms-crossed hero stance, no group line-ups. Neutral expression; an unprompted smile in conversation is not removed, but never directed |
| Ages | 20 to 60; at least 25% of technician images show someone over 40 |
| Gender | Gender-neutral: real team, equal dignity. Invite women technicians and trainers; no tokenism, and never women only as customers |
| Regions | Spread across Punjab, Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Jharkhand, Maharashtra, tier-2 and tier-3 towns |
| Customers | Hands, shoes at a doorway, a signature; faces only with consent and never as the subject. No family portraits, no smiling-with-certificate shots |
| Skin, body, headwear | Never lighten skin; varied bodies; turbans and other religious headwear shown naturally, PPE per SOP, never retouched |
| Dignity | Technician and customer at the same eye level; never kneeling, never shot from above |
| Team portraits | Allowed only as plain ID-style frames for a team page: neutral expression, flat light, stone `ct.color.neutral.50` backdrop. Not heroes |

Stereotype blacklist: poverty tourism, faceless grimy "labourer", technician as servant, only-metro or only-light-skinned or only-male casts, thumbs-up hero, grinning at camera, scared parent holding a glass of water, tricolour or festival staging. Indian screen casts skew strongly to light skin (Geena Davis Institute, TV and ad data): use as a caution.

| Mode | Meaning | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Candid | Captured during a real job | Default, about 70% |
| Staged-real | Real technician, equipment and site; step repeated for camera, done safely, still in working posture | Allowed, about 30%; never captioned as a named customer's job |
| Constructed | Actors or mock sets shown as a real job | Not allowed publicly |

## 08.5 PPE and safety realism

A photo must never show a method we would not permit on a real job. **Confirm every row with the operations safety SOP before the shoot.** The SOP is not in the project files, so these are general realism rules, not company procedure.

| Situation | Shown correctly | Never shown |
|---|---|---|
| Confined-space entry | Standby person at the opening, entry gear and lifeline clipped to an anchor, ventilation running, entry only if SOP allows (confirm with operations safety SOP) | Solo entry, head-first lean-in, phone in hand |
| Head, face, eyes | Helmet, mask or respirator, eye protection for jetting, disinfection and UV as SOP requires (confirm with operations safety SOP) | Bare-faced agent spraying in a closed space, UV aimed at a person |
| Hands and feet | Gloves, safety shoes on the job; shoes off only when entering a home | Flip-flops, bare feet at the tank |
| Jet, hoses, cables | Lance aimed into the tank, hose clear of walkways, cables dry and raised | Jet at a person, cables in standing water |
| Behaviour | Calm, deliberate | Smoking, stunts, selfies |

PPE keeps its real colours; it is never graded toward navy or coral. Helmet and PPE styling belongs to s16.

## 08.6 Composition, framing and ratios

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Camera height | Subject eye level (about 1.5 m); hands at 45 degrees; roofs 1.4 to 1.6 m |
| Level | Horizon within 1 degree; verticals straight; frame lines parallel to the image edge |
| Negative space | 40% or more of every still left empty (plain wall, sky, concrete, water surface); 55% or more on hero plates |
| Placement | One subject on one side; 5% margin on every side; subject aligned to a 12-column grid third (cols 8 to 12 or 1 to 5) |
| Documents | Flat 90 degrees or 45 degrees, never skewed |
| Depth of field | Moderate: background readable; no bokeh look |
| Stability | Tripod at sites; handheld at 1/250 s or faster |

## 08.7 Lighting recipe

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Source | Daylight in open shade or overcast; large, soft |
| Time | 08:30 to 11:00 and 15:30 to 17:30; avoid 11:00 to 15:30 hard sun on roofs unless in shade or under a 1.1 m diffuser |
| Colour temperature | 5200 to 5800 K, set from a grey card at each location |
| Key to fill | 2:1 to 3:1, white reflector or bounce, never flash |
| Direction | Key at 45 degrees, from the side of the working hand |
| Underground or indoor | Daylight LED panels, 5600 K, CRI 95 or higher; one soft source plus one fill; no coloured gels; no warm bulbs left in frame |
| ISO ceiling | 1600 daylight, 3200 sumps |
| Forbidden | On-camera flash, rim light, backlight haze, lens flare, sun rays, bloom or glow, diffusion filters, added steam or smoke, wet-look specular highlights |

## 08.8 Colour grade: "Clean Tank Daylight v2"

Muted, neutral, honest. Lower saturation than v1, whites neutral to slightly warm so they sit with the stone neutral (`ct.color.neutral.50`), deeper navy, flat tonality with no glow. Not teal-orange, not cinematic, not moody, not golden, not cool-blue.

| Setting (Lightroom or Camera Raw, starting values) | v2 value | Change from v1 |
|---|---|---|
| Files | `ctd-v2.xmp` preset and `ctd-v2.cube` LUT | renamed |
| White balance | From grey card; white patch R, G, B within 4 of each other with R minus B of +2 to +5 (matches stone `#F5F4F1`, R minus B = 4); never cool | was B minus R 0 to +4 |
| Exposure | White wall or tank at RGB 232 to 242, no clipped texture | 3 lower, softer whites |
| Contrast / Highlights / Shadows | +3 / -25 / +10 | calmer |
| Whites / Blacks | 0 / -3 (black point RGB 10 to 16, not milky, not crushed) | |
| Texture / Clarity / Dehaze | +8 / 0 / 0 | clarity off, texture up for concrete and steel |
| Vibrance / Saturation | -5 / -12 | was 0 / -6 |
| HSL Red and Orange | Hue 0, Saturation -8 | |
| HSL Blue | Hue 0, Saturation -4, Luminance -14 (deeper navy) | was Sat 0, Lum -8 |
| HSL Aqua and Teal | Saturation -30 | was -20 |
| Colour grading | Shadows hue 220, saturation 3; highlights hue 40, saturation 2; midtones 0 | highlights slightly warm |
| Sharpen / Noise | 40, radius 1.0, detail 25 / luminance 10 above ISO 1600 | |
| Vignette, grain, fade, split-tone, glow, bloom, negative clarity | Off | glow added to ban list |

| Pass check | Target |
|---|---|
| Frame brightness | Mean luminance 42 to 58 of 100 |
| Clipping | Highlights under 1% of area, shadows under 0.5% |
| White patch | R at most 5 above B, G between R and B; sits visibly with stone, not blue-white |
| Saturation | Mean HSL saturation of the frame at most 30%, except the navy uniform |
| Navy uniform (lit mid-tone) | HSL hue 205 to 225, saturation 25 to 50%, lightness 12 to 28% (flat swatch `#20334B` is hue 213, 40%, 21%) |
| Skin | On the vectorscope skin line (about 20 to 50 degrees); checked against Fitzpatrick I to VI; no lightening, no orange cast |
| Accent objects | Hue stays 8 to 18, never saffron or pink |
| Glow | No halo around highlights or edges at 100% zoom |

Grade test sheet (build on the pilot): 4 scenes (rooftop in sun, rooftop in shade, sump under LED, corridor), each as three panels: as shot, Clean Tank Daylight v2, reject. ColorChecker frame at each location. Also test every hero plate under the scrim (08.10).

## 08.10 Website hero photo

The hero is a full-bleed background photo with the condensed-caps headline and CTAs on top (s07, A1). Header is fixed or floating (owner TBD, s07 H1 or H2). One photo, three art-directed crops; never a single image left to `object-fit: cover`.

| Item | Desktop | Tablet portrait | Phone portrait |
|---|---|---|---|
| Breakpoint | 1024 px and up | 480 to 767 px | below 480 px (360 reference) |
| Crop | **16:9**, 2400 x 1350 | **4:5**, 1080 x 1350 | **9:16**, 1080 x 1920 |
| Hero box (s07) | min height 640, full-bleed | min height 560 | min height 560 |
| Subject zone | Right 42% (cols 8 to 12); vertically 20% to 85% | Top 45%, centred | 12% to 45% of height, centred |
| Text zone | Left 58% (cols 1 to 7): H1, one line, CTAs, ref tag | Lower 50% | Lower 50%, left-aligned, 16 px gutter |
| Empty space | 55% or more (plain concrete, wall, sky) | 50% | 45% |
| Width set (`srcset`) | 1600 / 2400 | 768 / 1080 | 480 / 720 / 1080 |

Source plates P24 to P26 are shot at 6000 px wide with the subject wholly inside the right 42% and extra headroom, so all three crops come from one frame. Set `object-position` per crop; export formats per 08.14. A still poster only; no autoplay video (s07).

**Scrim (flat, no gradient).** Colour `ct.color.primary.900` `#0F1824`, opacity `ct.scrim.hero` = **70%**, over the whole hero photo. White on `#0F1824` alone is 17.85:1. Composite ratios below are white text over the scrim on the lightest possible photo (pure white, RGB 255) and on graded highlights.

| Scrim opacity | Over white photo | Over stone `#F5F4F1` | Over `#C7C6C1` | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50% | 3.39:1 | 3.67:1 | 5.07:1 | fails body |
| 55% | 3.98:1 | 4.27:1 | 5.78:1 | fails body |
| 60% | 4.70:1 | 4.99:1 | 6.55:1 | floor, passes 4.5:1 on any photo |
| 65% | 5.54:1 | 5.87:1 | 7.55:1 | passes |
| **70%** | **6.64:1** | 6.96:1 | 8.68:1 | **default**, margin for Devanagari and Gurmukhi thin strokes |
| 75% | 7.90:1 | 8.30:1 | 9.87:1 | passes, photo mostly hidden |

Use 70%. Range 60 to 75% is GUARD; below 60% is never allowed. At 70% the photo shows through at about 30%, so choose plates for strong structure (tank edge, hose line, gauge dial, concrete joints), not tonal subtlety.

**Header overlap zone.** Header height 64 px fixed (56 phone) or 72 px floating (s07). The top 12% of every crop (72 px of a 640 px hero is 11.25%) is the overlap zone:
- **Fixed solid header (H1):** photo starts below the bar; the zone is not in the photo. Keep top 5% margin clear anyway.
- **Floating header (H2):** transparent over the photo at page top; the bar sits on the scrim. The zone holds no face, hands, gauge or bright object, only sky, wall or concrete. White nav text meets 6.64:1 (worst case). Becomes solid `ct.color.primary.700` after 80 px of scroll.
- The scrim extends under the header zone in both options. `scroll-padding-top` equals header height (s07).

**Hero pass test.** (1) Subject and its key object sit inside the subject zone in all three crops. (2) Text zone is plain enough that no edge or bright patch crosses a letter. (3) Brightest 5% of pixels behind the text, blended at 70%, give white text 4.5:1 or better (sample per letter, including the Hindi and Punjabi headline). (4) No accent under 1% rule broken. (5) Checked at 360 px, in dark mode and as a 120 px thumbnail.

## 08.11 Before and after policy

Allowed only as honest documentation, never as shock.

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Use | Service record, franchise training, internal audit; public only with written customer consent |
| Pairing | Same position, lens, height, light, crop and grade; no retouching |
| Dirt | Never add, enhance, stage or pick the worst corner |
| Tone | Neutral angle; no arrows, red circles or fear captions |
| Caption | States only what was done and when: "Overhead tank interior, before and after cleaning, [city], 03 Oct 2026" |
| Hygiene | If implied, add the qualifier: water quality also depends on source water, plumbing, tank condition and later maintenance, which are outside our service |

## 08.12 Consent, privacy, releases and rights

An identifiable photograph is personal data under the DPDP Act 2023, and consent is one of several processing grounds, so need depends on facts. **Clean Tank rule: written consent for every identifiable person, always; counsel to verify.** The DPDP Rules 2025 are reported as notified in November 2025 with phased compliance (verify dates).

| Paper | Used for | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Model release | Any identifiable technician, partner, customer | Signed, covers channels and territory; Consent ID in metadata |
| Minors | Any child | Policy: no identifiable children; if ever needed a guardian signs (counsel to verify) |
| Site permission | Homes, societies, hospitals, schools | Signed by owner, RWA or in-charge; defence, railway, government and hospital sites may restrict photography |
| Before/after consent | Public tank interiors | Per image and channel |
| Client name or logo | LIC, MES, BSNL, AIIMS, IIT Kanpur, others | Permission before any public use; until then frame so the site is not identifiable |
| Photographer agreement | Hired photographers | Written copyright assignment to Gobind Galaxy; Copyright Act 1957 s.17(b) default may favour the commissioner but assignment removes doubt (counsel to confirm) |
| Partner photos | Franchise partners' jobs | Signed licence to Gobind Galaxy; terms per franchise agreement (counsel to confirm) |

Never visible in a public image: house or flat numbers, nameplates, society names without permission, others' vehicle plates, residents' or neighbours' faces without consent, documents with names, family photographs, religious items, valuables, CCTV screens. A public sample certificate uses fictional data labelled "Sample". Retention period for releases: open.

## 08.15 Mobile phone guide for franchise partners

Six photos per job, one phone, one preset.

| Setting | Do |
|---|---|
| Lens | Rear 1x; 2x for people and detail; no digital zoom beyond 2x; wipe the lens |
| Format | JPEG "most compatible", highest resolution |
| Mode | Photo; grid on; flash off; portrait mode, beauty and filters off |
| Orientation | Horizontal; vertical only for Stories |
| Exposure | Tap the white tank wall, lower by 0.3 to 0.7 so white does not blow out |
| Light | Shade or overcast; sun behind you at 45 degrees; never sky behind the subject |
| Angle and distance | Eye level, straight horizon, chest-height two-hand hold; take wide, medium and close; leave a third of the frame empty |
| Edit | Crop only, then apply the `Clean Tank Daylight v2` mobile preset (verify Lightroom Mobile free-tier support) |

Six photos every job: (1) arrival in uniform, (2) readiness checklist, (3) one process step with a person at work, (4) dated sticker on the tank, (5) certificate handover (hands only), (6) clean tank after service, open and dated.

Never shoot: faces without consent, children, posed or smiling-to-camera group shots, house numbers, documents with names, a technician in a tank without PPE or standby, UV aimed at people, shock close-ups of sludge, selfies in the manhole, filter or emoji overlays, competitors' tanks, client buildings without permission. **Safety first; never delay or alter a safe procedure for a photo.** Upload within 24 hours with the naming pattern and a signed release for anyone visible.

## 08.19 Accessibility & India notes

- Informative photos carry alt text in English, Hindi and Punjabi (`alt_en`, `alt_hi`, `alt_pa`); decorative ones (a hero with the headline in live text) have empty alt or a short descriptive alt, per s07.
- Text over photos reaches 4.5:1 body and 3:1 large (WCAG 1.4.3), sampled per letter. Devanagari and Gurmukhi have thin strokes and may wrap to 2 or 3 lines: keep them large, at the 70% scrim, and follow the s04 weights. Plex Mono has no Indic glyphs, so Indic ref tags use Anek.
- Meaning never depends on a colour in a photo; keep flashing or busy images away from UI.
- Show Indian realities: rooftop plastic and RCC tanks, sumps, societies, mixed-script signage; all skin tones, ages, genders and regions as everyday life. Removing shoes at a home is part of the brand story (P02, shown by the doorway, not a face).
- Slow networks: hero responsive sizes, AVIF or WebP, a still poster, no autoplay video (s07).
- Written permission first at defence, railway, government and hospital sites.
- Accent (hue 13) is not saffron: never stage orange, white and green together.
- North India midday is harsh and dusty: shoot early and late.

# Clean Tank Services — Phase 9
# Brand Architecture & Naming

## 9.1 Architecture

**Gobind Galaxy**\
Parent Company

↓

**Clean Tank Services**\
Customer-facing Master / Operating Brand

↓

**Scientific & Mechanized Water Tank Cleaning**\
Permanent Category Descriptor

↓

**Authorised Service Provider Network**\
Local franchise / territory operators

## 9.2 Master Brand

The primary market-facing identity is:

**CLEAN TANK SERVICES**

## 9.3 Permanent Descriptor

The preferred descriptor is:

**Scientific & Mechanized Water Tank Cleaning**

This immediately communicates both the category and the intended method
of service.

## 9.4 Tagline

**Clean Tank, Healthy Life.**

The tagline and descriptor serve different purposes and should not be
treated as interchangeable.

## 9.5 Franchise Naming Architecture

A local franchise should present itself primarily through the Clean Tank
brand.

Recommended structure:

**CLEAN TANK SERVICES**\
*Scientific & Mechanized Water Tank Cleaning*\
**Authorised Service Provider**\
**\[Franchisee Name\] \| \[City / District\]**

The franchisee's own identity may identify the local operator, but the
primary service brand remains Clean Tank Services.

## 9.6 One Brand, Many Local Operators, One Standard

The national network should create the impression of one professional
brand operating through many local authorised service providers.

Brand consistency should extend beyond the logo to:

-   Uniforms
-   Equipment presentation
-   Customer communication
-   Service process
-   Documentation
-   Certificates
-   Marketing materials
-   Customer handling
-   Quality expectations

## 9.7 Expansion Rule

Clean Tank Services should remain focused exclusively on
water-storage-tank cleaning.

It should not dilute its specialist positioning by expanding into
unrelated tank categories or becoming a generic cleaning company.

Where future offerings are genuinely within the brand's core
water-storage-tank expertise, they should normally remain under Clean
Tank Services rather than creating unnecessary sub-brands.

# Clean Tank Services — Phase 6
# Personality & Archetype

## 6.1 Personality Foundation

Clean Tank Services should feel like a highly knowledgeable professional
who is confident in their expertise but remains humble in behaviour.

The brand is:

-   Knowledgeable
-   Professional
-   Scientific
-   Mechanized
-   Disciplined
-   Honest
-   Respectful
-   Soft-spoken
-   Humble
-   Responsible
-   Trustworthy
-   Process-driven
-   Experienced
-   Approachable

## 6.2 The Human Character of Clean Tank

If Clean Tank were a person, that person would be experienced enough to
know exactly what they are doing, knowledgeable enough to explain it
clearly, disciplined enough to complete the process properly, and humble
enough never to treat the customer with arrogance.

The person would not say:

> "We are busy, so finish quickly because we have somewhere else to go."

Instead, the person would respect the customer and complete the required
work properly.

The philosophy is captured by the idea:

**"The tree that bears fruit bends down."**

Knowledge and capability should create humility, not arrogance.

-   Humble
-   Honest

## 6.4 We Are Not

-   Arrogant
-   Dismissive
-   Rushed at the expense of quality
-   Careless about process
-   Overly complicated
-   Aggressively sales-driven
-   Pretentious
-   Informal to the point of unprofessionalism
-   A generic cleaning business
-   A brand that makes unsupported technical claims

## 6.5 Archetype Direction

Clean Tank's primary archetypal character is the **Expert / Sage**:
knowledge, competence, explanation, process understanding and informed
guidance.

Its supporting character is the **Caregiver**: responsibility, respect,
hygiene awareness and genuine concern for the customer's environment.

The archetypes are expressed practically rather than theatrically. Clean
Tank does not present itself as an authority for authority's sake. It
earns trust through knowledge, process and behaviour.

## 6.6 Core Personality Principle

**Expertise without arrogance.\
Professionalism without distance.\
Knowledge without unnecessary complexity.\
Service without pressure.**

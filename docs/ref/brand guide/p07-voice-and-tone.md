# Clean Tank Services — Phase 7
# Voice & Tone

## 7.1 Brand Voice

Clean Tank's voice should be:

-   Clear
-   Knowledgeable
-   Professional
-   Respectful
-   Practical
-   Reassuring
-   Educational
-   Simple enough for ordinary customers
-   Technically responsible

## 7.2 Communication Rule

Clean Tank should communicate like an expert who knows the subject well
and wants the customer to understand it.

Technical knowledge should be simplified where necessary, not hidden and
not unnecessarily complicated.

## 7.3 Tone

Tone can change according to context, but the underlying character must
remain consistent.

### Customer Enquiry

Helpful, clear and practical.

### Service Communication

Confident, organised and respectful.

### Educational Content

Informative and easy to understand.

### Corporate / Institutional Communication

Formal, precise and evidence-based.

### Complaint Handling

Calm, respectful, accountable and solution-oriented.

### Franchise Communication

Structured, supportive, professional and standard-driven.

## 7.4 Communication Boundaries

The brand should avoid:

-   Unverified claims
-   Exaggerated superiority
-   Fear-based selling
-   Unnecessary technical jargon
-   Disrespectful language
-   Pressure tactics
-   Claims that cannot be supported by evidence

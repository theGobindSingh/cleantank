# Clean Tank Services — Phase 8
# Messaging

## 8.1 Master Message

**Clean Tank Services provides scientific and mechanized
water-storage-tank cleaning through knowledgeable people, disciplined
processes and professional execution.**

## 8.2 Core Messaging Pillars

### Pillar 1 --- Scientific & Mechanized Cleaning

Clean Tank uses a defined scientific and mechanized approach rather than
treating tank cleaning as an informal washing activity.

### Pillar 2 --- Knowledgeable & Professional People

The people representing Clean Tank should understand the work they
perform and be able to explain the process properly.

### Pillar 3 --- Complete Process & Discipline

The objective is not to finish quickly. The required process must be
completed properly, even when the schedule is busy.

### Pillar 4 --- Trust & Hygiene Awareness

The service should leave the customer with confidence, while the brand
also helps people understand why water-storage-tank hygiene matters.

## 8.3 Customer Memory

After interacting with Clean Tank, the customer should remember:

**"These people know their work, follow a proper process, behave well
and can be trusted with my water-storage tank."**

## 8.4 Tagline

**Clean Tank, Healthy Life.**

## 8.5 Brand Descriptor

**Scientific & Mechanized Water Tank Cleaning**

## 8.6 Elevator Pitch

Clean Tank Services is a specialist water-storage-tank cleaning brand of
Gobind Galaxy. We provide scientific and mechanized tank cleaning
through knowledgeable professionals, disciplined processes and a
customer-focused approach, helping homes and institutions maintain
better water-storage hygiene.

## 8.7 Claims Discipline

All technical, regulatory, chemical, certification or health-related
claims must be supported by appropriate documentation before being used
in public-facing communication.

The brand should never convert an internal belief or aspiration into an
externally stated certification, approval or scientific claim without
evidence.

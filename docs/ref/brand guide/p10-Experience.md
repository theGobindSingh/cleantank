# Clean Tank Services — Phase 10
# Brand Experience

## 10.1 Experience Principle

**Professional in the system, simple in the customer experience.**

The customer journey should be systematic internally but uncomplicated
externally.

## 10.2 Enquiry Channels

Customers may reach Clean Tank through different channels depending on
the situation:

-   Phone
-   WhatsApp
-   Website
-   Online enquiry
-   Other legitimate service channels

The channel may vary; the quality and professionalism of the experience
should not.

## 10.3 Initial Requirement Capture

Before scheduling service, Clean Tank should collect the practical
information needed to plan the job, including:

-   Customer name
-   Location
-   Tank type
-   Tank capacity
-   Approximate cleaning requirement
-   Time since last cleaning
-   Other information necessary for planning

The objective is to understand the job sufficiently to send the
appropriate people and resources.

## 10.4 Avoiding Unnecessary Friction

For ordinary domestic water-storage tanks, the process should not be
made unnecessarily complicated through avoidable inspection visits.

Where adequate information can be collected remotely, the service should
be planned from that information.

## 10.5 Service Execution

The customer should experience:

-   Professional arrival
-   Respectful interaction
-   Knowledgeable personnel
-   Proper equipment
-   Defined scientific and mechanized process
-   Complete execution
-   No deliberate rushing at the expense of process quality

## 10.6 Post-Service Experience

After cleaning:

-   The customer receives a **Cleaning Certificate**.
-   A **Cleaning Date Sticker** is placed on the tank.
-   The sticker records the cleaning date and recommended/due date.
-   The customer is informed of the recommended cleaning cycle.
-   The customer remains free to decide when to undertake the next
    service.

## 10.7 Customer Database

A structured customer database should maintain relevant service
information, such as:

-   Customer name
-   Location
-   Tank capacity/type
-   Cleaning date
-   Due/recommended date
-   Service history

## 10.8 Renewal / Follow-up System

The system can generate an internal reminder around the recommended
cleaning cycle.

A practical follow-up can be made approximately one week before the
expected due date.

The customer should be asked whether they would like to schedule the
service.

The system may recommend approximately three-month cleaning intervals,
but customer circumstances can differ. Customers may choose a longer
interval such as six months depending on their needs and budget.

The system recommends; it does not pressure.

## 10.9 AMC & Accessibility

AMC customers may receive structured discounts according to the
company's commercial policy.

Over time, Clean Tank may also develop a controlled support mechanism
for customers who genuinely want the service but face financial
difficulty. Any free or subsidised service allocation should be governed
by a defined internal policy rather than presented as an unlimited
public guarantee.

## 10.10 Experience Outcome

The intended outcome is not merely:

**"The tank was cleaned."**

It is:

**"The tank was cleaned properly, I understand what was done, I have a
record of it, and I know whom to contact next time."**

A useful internal experience principle is:

**"We leave behind a record of the service, not just a cleaned tank."**

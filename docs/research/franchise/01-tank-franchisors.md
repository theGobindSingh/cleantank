## 3. The category's standard pitch

Every page is short, static, and built from the same parts:
1. "Be a [Brand] franchisee" hero, no promise in the headline.
2. Tank cleaning is a "need based", essential, recurring service.
3. Health hook: contaminated water, waterborne disease.
4. "Low investment, high returns" / "minimum investment".
5. Social standing: "wide social recognition", "Social Entrepreneur Program", "community well-being".
6. Technology label: "German", "European", "non-invasive", "zero human touch", "6/7 steps".
7. Claimed status: "India's no.1", "north India's no-1", "fastest growing", "first".
8. A benefits list: marketing kit, HQ training, stationery printing, help with government/corporate orders, troubleshooting.
9. Eligibility: "graduate preferred", "attitude for service", no experience needed.
10. ISO 9001 badge.
11. A single number: an investment range, sometimes ROI and payback (mostly only on directories).
12. One CTA to a generic contact form or WhatsApp.

Rarely or never shown: royalty basis, a fee breakdown, what the money buys item by item, unit economics, territory size, term renewal cost, exit terms, a named franchisee, a dated photo of a working unit.

Emotional hooks, quoted: "have experts working for you while you reap the profit" (Fastclean); "Earn Life Time" (Glowtank); "A Recession Proof Business Opportunity" (Crystal Clean; Mr. TEMS founder); "a golden opportunity for new entrepreneurs" (GD); "Low Investment High Returns" (Glowtank, Johnson Tanclean); "Earning Opportunity ₹1 Lakh Monthly*" (Hanbaz). No one uses "be your own boss" or government mandates directly; GD leans on disease awareness.

## 5. Red flags and weak spots

- Contradictory numbers on the same brand: Fastclean (four investment figures, two royalties, "No Investment"); Glowtank (four investment ranges, 2 vs 3 yr term, "0 units" vs "Exclusive Territories"); Easy Tank Clean (live terms on a "discontinued" programme; ₹2 lakh vs ₹30 lakh across directories) [listing].
- Implausible returns: Fastclean "₹24 lakh/month sales, 65% margin" on ₹5–6 lakh; Easy Tank Clean 140% ROI and 3–4 month payback; MannuBhai "100% Success Rate", "95% ROI"; TANKRO "Performance guarantee to unit franchisees: Yes" [listing].
- Fake or missing proof: Lorem Ipsum testimonials (Crystal Clean), "John Doe" comments and Lorem Ipsum bio (GD), no franchisee named anywhere.
- Stale pages: Crystal Clean © 2015, Glowtank © 2020, Fastclean media 2017, Johnson Tanclean review 2018; easytankclean.com and mytankclean dead.
- Copied copy: Glowtank's franchise page duplicates Fastclean's [inference].
- Hidden contacts: Franchise India hides every brand's phone behind OTP and paid upgrade, so directory leads go to the directory first.
- Equipment honesty: Mr. TEMS says its machinery cost ~₹8 lakh while inviting ₹4 lakh franchisees (prior notes).
- Complaints: none found for any brand in English searches ("Fastclean franchise review complaint fraud", Quora/Reddit). The search tool returned only US cases. This is weak evidence of absence; consumer courts, MouthShut, Google reviews and YouTube comments were not reachable through this tool.

## 7. Gaps: where an honest franchisor can stand out

Nobody does these; all are open to us [inference]:
1. Say what the royalty is charged on, in one line, with a worked example (3.5% of ₹X monthly billing = ₹Y). Nobody does; Fastclean contradicts itself.
2. Break the ₹3 lakh and ₹8 lakh models into a line-item kit list (machines, pumps, UV, PPE, uniforms, van or no van, launch marketing) with what the franchisee buys separately. Nobody publishes one.
3. Define ROI and payback: on what, over what, with what job volume and price. Show the assumptions; keep the mandatory disclaimer beside them. Fix our own 40%/4–6 months mismatch first.
4. Show real, dated proof that rivals fake: since 2008, institutional work orders and performance certificates (IIT Ropar, NIT Jalandhar, AIIMS Vijaypur etc. per INFO-BANK §7), real job photos, a named founder (the CEO).
5. Show the training plan by day and where it happens, and one sample of the operations manual or SOP.
6. Publish what leads look like (where they come from, how they are routed) without promising a number.
7. State plainly what we do not offer: no guaranteed income, no exclusivity, and why. Every rival either claims exclusivity or is silent; candour reads as confidence.
8. Answer the buyer's real FAQ: royalty basis, renewal fee, exit and transfer, what happens if I stop, who owns customers and AMC contracts, GST, whether a van is required, staff needed.
9. A qualifying form like GD's (city, investment band, timeframe, time to call) plus WhatsApp, with an ungated brochure.
10. Hindi and Punjabi franchise pages: none exist in the category.
11. A dated "last updated" stamp: every rival page is stale.
12. Institutional-sales support (tenders, GeM, work orders): Fastclean and Glowtank say "help getting government orders" in one line; we can show we actually win them.

## 8. Objections

Against us, from a buyer comparing:
- "₹8 lakh is the top of the market. Fastclean/TANKRO start at ₹2 lakh." Answer with the line-item kit list.
- "No exclusivity? Fastclean, Swachh, Clean Revolution, Glowtank, Wash India all give it."
- "How many franchisees do you have?" Rivals claim 20–50; we claim none.
- "Why only 40% ROI when others say 90–140%?" and "4–6 month payback contradicts 40%."
- "3.5% per month of what?"
- "You're new to franchising" (if true) against Fastclean since 2012.
- "Crystal Clean / Mr. TEMS is also in Mohali and offers a commercial tier."

Ours against them:
- Their numbers contradict across their own listings; ours are one set, in writing.

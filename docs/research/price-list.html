Cheap offers and GST

  • We will always cost more than the ₹399 to ₹500 offers. Against a ₹500 job our ₹708 is 1.4 times and our ₹944 is 1.9 times. The research says don't chase them.
  • GST is only compulsory above ₹20 lakh a year in sales: about 2,500 jobs at ₹800, or 7 a day. A one-van rival's ₹800 is likely final; ours becomes ₹944.
  • No rival page says whether GST is included. Good Life Future, a Tricity advertiser, sells on giving a proper GST bill, so a real bill helps with some buyers.
  • We never criticise plumbers or labourers, and the website never names a rival or prints a price comparison.

Societies and tenders

  • Government picks the lowest bid, priced from the rate book, tax included.
  • CCET, Sector 26: estimate ₹3,25,201, won at ₹2,63,738 (18.9% under) by a general contractor. The only public Tricity award with a winner.
  • NABI, Mohali: ₹5,70,896 for 3 years and 12 cleans, about ₹47,575 a clean.
  • Panjab University, 2024: ₹3,40,000 sumps, ₹4,75,000 overhead, re-advertised at the same estimate. Wants PWD, CPWD, MES or CHB listing; BSNL is not on the list.
  • Others: about ₹1 lakh High Court, ₹3.83 lakh MC Jandpur tank, ₹7.67 lakh IISER with sewage plants, ₹35 lakh MES 3 BRD with pipe work.
  • A society with two 1,00,000 L tanks cleaned twice a year: ₹52,000 before tax at ₹0.13 a litre (₹61,360 with GST), just above KPS's range.
  • Society AMC discounts elsewhere: 15% to 25% in Delhi, 20% to 30% in Bengaluru. IndiaMART's ₹0.20 to ₹0.60 and ₹1 a litre are mixed-unit listings, not tank prices.
  • A quote with GST inside gives 15.25% of it to tax. Some government and school work may be GST free; a CA must confirm.

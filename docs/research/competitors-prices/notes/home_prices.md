# Home water tank cleaning prices: Chandigarh Tricity and comparable Indian cities

Read on 31 Oct 2026. All figures are public listings. No vendor was contacted, no quote was requested, no form was submitted.

How to read the evidence grades used below:

- **A**: rate card read directly on the vendor or platform page, 31 Oct 2026.
- **B**: search-engine snippet only. The page itself blocks fetching (IndiaMART, Justdial and OLX returned 403 or timed out, including IndiaMART in a real browser). Date usually unknown, so treat as weaker.
- **C**: vendor blog or "typical cost" guide. Usually a marketing range, not a price a customer can book. Often made up.
- **D**: forum post by someone saying what they paid.

"GST: not stated" means the page does not say whether GST is included. It does not mean GST is included. Only a few pages mention GST at all.

## What prices are published for Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula, Zirakpur, Kharar and Punjab?

### Takeaway
Only three sources publish sized prices that reach the Tricity: Urban Company, HomeTriangle and Guga Aviation. Everything else local is a "from ₹499" teaser or no price at all, so the true local floor is about ₹500 for a small overhead tank. Urban Company is ₹949 in the Tricity, not ₹799.

### Inferences
- Tricity overhead market in practice has two levels: about ₹500 for informal or teaser pricing, and about ₹780 to ₹1,500 for a 1,000 L tank on sized cards.
- "From ₹499" pages have no size, so they likely apply to a small tank at best. They are bait prices until proven otherwise, but customers will still anchor on them.
- Guga's URL pattern (`/services/water-tank-cleaning/chandigarh/`) and aviation branding suggest a multi-city SEO page. Whether it really delivers this in the Tricity is unverified, so its numbers are best read as a ceiling.

## How is pricing structured?

### Takeaway
Homes are sold per tank by capacity band. Tank type is a real price axis everywhere except our list: RCC costs 16% to 54% more on HomeTriangle, and underground costs well above overhead. Per-litre pricing appears mainly on B2B listings, and per-sq-ft on none of the home sources.

### Inferences
- Our list prices a sump, an RCC tank and a Sintex at the same rate. Every sized competitor separates at least overhead from underground. That is simple to sell, but it gives away margin on sumps (see the last section).
- Our "small extra for a really hard-to-reach tank" fits market practice. Nobody publishes a figure.
- Multi-tank, worked out: two 1,000 L tanks in one visit cost ₹1,440 with us before GST (₹1,699 with GST), against ₹1,099 to ₹1,299 from KaamGenie Gurgaon. One 1,000 L overhead plus one 5,000 L sump costs ₹1,980 (₹2,336 with GST), against ₹2,200-3,000 from KaamGenie Delhi for a combined visit. Our percentage discount is weaker for a second tank of the same size than "second tank at ₹400-600", but better on overhead plus sump.

### Gaps
- No published rupee amount for height surcharge or sludge disposal from any source.

### Inferences
- Strongest position: small to mid tanks in Tricity at the Urban Company price point, with a mechanized method, and sumps. A 2,000 L sump at ₹1,180 with GST beats Urban Company's ₹1,699 by ₹519.
- Weakest position: against the ₹499-500 local floor, and on a 5,000 L overhead tank against metro cards, though those cards do not serve the Tricity.
- Same price for all tank types leaves sump and RCC well below what the market pays. If the owner ever wants to raise anything, this is the widest gap, though it would cost the "price by size alone" simplicity.
- Above 10,000 L the choice of benchmark decides the verdict. Against the metro cards we are double. Against the only sized cards reaching the Tricity we are at or below. Few homes have tanks this size, and metro cards at this size look thin.
- The 30,000 L inversion is the one clear error in the list. It looks unintended, and it is worth raising with the owner, for example a floor of ₹6,000 above 30,000 L or a marginal rate.

### Gaps
- Bands to 10,000 L overhead and above have 3 to 6 cards, so those medians are fragile.
- Band to 500 L: most cards do not have a separate 500 L band, so the ≤1,000 L price stands in, which likely overstates market there.
- Listed prices are not paid prices. No band has real Tricity transaction data.
- No band has any Tricity plumber data.

Research date: 31 Oct 2026. Every figure is tagged **[Estimate]** (buyer's tender estimate or schedule rate), **[Quoted]** (vendor list or listing), **[Awarded]** (contract or L1 result) or **[Calc]** (my arithmetic on stated capacity or area). Clean Tank's home list, for comparison: ₹0.20/L for 10,001 to 30,000 L and ₹0.13/L above that, before 18% GST ([INFO-BANK §Home prices](/home/ubuntu/Programming/ct/docs/ref/INFO-BANK.md)).

## 1. Government tenders, schedules of rates and awarded rates

### Takeaway
The Central government rate book does price this work. CPWD DSR gives ₹0.40/L (2021) for terrace tanks up to 2,000 L and ₹71.30 to ₹84.85 per sqm of inside surface for sumps and RCC overhead tanks. Tricity and Punjab tenders mostly publish a lump estimate without capacity, so only one Tricity award could be found (CCET Chandigarh, L1 18.9% below estimate), and no per-litre award exists in the public record I could reach.

### Inferences
- Size drives the unit price more than sector does. Per-litre rates fall from ₹0.35 to ₹0.60 at 500 to 2,000 L, to ₹0.17 to ₹0.35 at 2,000 to 10,000 L, to ₹0.02 to ₹0.09 for sumps of 2 lakh L and more. Sources differ in year and city, so read this as a shape, not a table.
- The sqm item is the honest bridge for big tanks. On plain cuboids at ₹84.85/sqm: 10,000 L (2.5×2×2 m, 28 sqm) is ₹2,376 or ₹0.24/L; 30,000 L (4×3×2.5 m, 59 sqm) is ₹5,006 or ₹0.17/L; 1,00,000 L (5×5×4 m, 130 sqm) is ₹11,030 or ₹0.11/L; 5,00,000 L (10×10×5 m, 400 sqm) is ₹33,940 or ₹0.07/L. MAKAUT's real 4.5 lakh L tank at 481 sqm fits this. These tanks are my illustration, not tender data.
- Against that: Clean Tank's ₹0.20/L for 10 to 30 kL matches DSR. Its flat ₹0.13/L is below DSR at 30 kL, level at about 70 to 80 kL, and about double it at 5 lakh L (₹65,000 against ₹33,940). Home-list small tanks run above DSR: ₹600 for 500 L against ₹240, ₹800 for 1,000 L against ₹480, about level at 2,000 L (₹1,000 against ₹960).
- DSR rates include the GST effect and estimates are not awards, so a real L1 sits lower. One Tricity data point shows 19% below estimate.
- IIM Lucknow's ₹98,906 estimate is close to Clean Tank's own ₹96,445 in 2020 and about ₹91,000 per year in the 2021-23 AMC (₹1,82,860 over two years). If the 2024 inventory was the same scope, the firm billed around 5 to 6 paise per litre there, well under its home list. That scope match is my assumption; the owner can check it. IIML paid per tank, and the tender says so: "per type and no. of Tanks cleaned and not per liter".
- NABI's value is probably near ₹5.7 lakh for three years, since EMD was exactly 2.0% of value in the five tenders I could test (CCET, Jandpur, CUP, both IIT Delhi). Unconfirmed.
- ₹41,300 at the Mint equals ₹35,000 plus 18% exactly, which suggests GST was charged to a government unit.

## 2. Who wins, and which qualification rules recur

### Takeaway
Small general contractors win Tricity work on L1, and specialists are rarely visible. The recurring gates are GST and PAN, ESI and EPF, similar-work value (the 80/60/40 or 80/50/40 rule), three years of ITR or balance sheet, and sometimes CPWD or MES enlistment. No document I read asked for ISO.

### Cited Findings
- CCET Chandigarh: L1 M/s Dinesh Pandey; L2 M/S Kite Elevators, ₹3,16,973, rejected. TenderShark's "20.2% below L2" is mislabelled: L2 was 20.2% above L1, and L1 was 16.8% below L2 [Calc] — [TenderShark](https://www.tendershark.com/details/chandigarh-tender/administration/3292226b-dd9b-47f4-8242-d44ef3b442d1)
- A search for "Dinesh Pandey" found nothing else on the contractor — searched 31 Oct 2026
- Central University of Punjab: CPWD or MES or central enlistment, or simply valid GST; one similar work of 80% value, two of 50% or three of 40% in the last 3 years; CA balance sheet or ITR for 3 years; non-blacklisting affidavit; integrity pact; PAN. EMD 2% of tendered value, performance guarantee 5%, quantities can vary 25%, tank cleaning register kept — [CUP tender](https://cup.edu.in/sites/default/files/NITWTC.pdf)
- IIT Delhi hostels: registered with CPWD, MES, BSNL or Railways in the relevant class; one similar work ≥ ₹7,59,000, two ≥ ₹5,69,000 or three ≥ ₹3,80,000 in last 7 years; bidder rejected if ESI and EPF registration is not uploaded; EPFO, ESIC and BOCW papers needed before any running bill; EMD ₹18,960 with no MSME relaxation; no joint ventures — [IIT Delhi NIT](https://home.iitd.ac.in/public/storage/tenders/NIT_1792576541.pdf). West Campus counts building maintenance and new construction as similar work, so general contractors qualify — [IIT Delhi West](https://home.iitd.ac.in/public/storage/tenders_archives/NIT_1789386390.pdf)
- IIM Lucknow: average turnover at least ₹2 lakh; experience with government, PSU, IIM, IIT or NIT; 5 years with mechanical equipment and WHO-listed food-grade chemicals; EMD ₹4,000; MSME fee exemption; testing at a Govt or NABL lab at contractor's cost; before and after photographs — [IIML tender](https://www.iiml.ac.in/sites/default/files/upload/tender/1817788013Revised.pdf)
- LNMIIT Jaipur (private deemed university, 8,50,000 L, 2022): PAN, GST, three-year turnover, completion certificates, client list — [LNMIIT](https://login.lnmiit.ac.in/WebAPI/downloadTender/458)
- MAKAUT: registered firm, state office, 3 years with government bodies, no subcontracting — [MAKAUT](https://www.makautwb.ac.in/datas/users/0-tender_undergroundtank25.pdf)
- SPAV: PAN and GST on letterhead, sealed post — [SPAV](https://spav.ac.in/2026/tender/NIQ-Water-tank-cleaning-services.pdf)
- A text search of the CUP, IIT Delhi, IIML and LNMIIT documents found no ISO requirement — documents above, checked 31 Oct 2026
- Clean Tank holds GST, EPFO, ESI, MSME/Udyam, BSNL enlistment and ISO 9001:2015 — [INFO-BANK](/home/ubuntu/Programming/ct/docs/ref/INFO-BANK.md)

### Inferences
- Clean Tank clears the common gates. Its certificates list several ₹5 to 7 lakh orders, enough for the similar-work test on Tricity jobs under about ₹8 lakh.
- The threat in the Tricity is price from general contractors rather than qualification. An elevator firm bid on CCET.

## 4. How big tanks are priced, and the extras

### Takeaway
Big-tank pricing uses four units: per sqm or sq ft of inside surface (DSR, MAKAUT), per tank with a capacity stated (IIML, ACTREC), per litre (SPAV, IIT Delhi, CUP's small tanks) and percentage on estimate (CUP, CCET). Buyers usually roll testing, sludge, disposal, all heights and GST into the rate. No source prices night or holiday work.

### Cited Findings
- Sludge, disposal and heights are included: IIT Delhi says rates "apply for all heights & depths, Leads & Lifts" and are GST-inclusive — [IIT Delhi](https://home.iitd.ac.in/public/storage/tenders/NIT_1792576541.pdf); DSR items include disposal of silt and rubbish — [DSR 2021](https://cpmgsupune.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/DSR2021-Vol-II.pdf)
- Testing is on the contractor: IIML "testing charges... shall be borne entirely by the Contractor", at Govt or NABL labs — [IIML](https://www.iiml.ac.in/sites/default/files/upload/tender/1817788013Revised.pdf)
- Penalty: ₹0.10/L deducted if the date is not stencilled — [DSR 2021](https://cpmgsupune.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/DSR2021-Vol-II.pdf)
- Staging premium appears in scope only: IIML's main overhead tank is priced as its own item at 8 m height on 20 m staging — [IIML](https://www.iiml.ac.in/sites/default/files/upload/tender/1817788013Revised.pdf)
- Vendor surcharges named without amounts: sludge, travel, high-pressure equipment, night or emergency, rooftop access with no lift — [AR price guide](https://www.arwatertankcleaners.in/water-tank-cleaning-price/)
- Water test prices [Quoted], and they conflict: Envirochem (Panipat lab, on PPCB's site) IS 10500 essential parameters ₹1,500, complete ₹5,500 per sample, coliform ₹1,500, undated — [PPCB-hosted list](https://ppcb.punjab.gov.in/media/documents/Envirochem.pdf); STIC microbial ₹1,500, or ₹2,000 for non-domestic, from 1 Jun 2025 — [STIC](https://sticindia.com/resources/Analysis%20Charges%20-%20from%2001-06-2025.pdf) (search snippet); full IS 10500 panel ₹800 to ₹3,000+ ([Studio Matrx](https://www.studiomatrx.org/guides/water-quality-testing-india)) against ₹5,000 to ₹15,000 ([Auriga](https://aurigaresearchsaurabh.pages.dev/testing-charges/)) (snippets)

### Inferences
- A public bid must carry testing, disposal and GST inside the rate. The list price needs to be built that way before it is compared with a tender.
- On a ₹98,906 AMC like IIML's, one complete IS 10500 test at ₹5,500 is over 5% of contract value, so "all testing included" should be scoped by sample count.

### Gaps
- No published night or holiday rate, no sludge-volume rate (per cum or per tonne), and no Chandigarh or Mohali NABL lab price. The Tribune report on MC water testing charges could not be read.

## 6. GST on tank cleaning, and government bodies

### Takeaway
18% is the normal rate (9% CGST + 9% SGST or UTGST), and three exemptions can make it Nil: municipal-function work for government, water supply or public health or sanitation for a Governmental Authority, and cleaning performed in schools up to higher secondary. Universities, IITs and hospitals are treated as taxable in the tenders read.

### Cited Findings
- SAC 998534 "Specialized cleaning services for reservoirs and tanks", 18%, unchanged across the 22 Sep 2025 revision — [Busy, third party](https://busy.in/sac-code-998534)
- Heading 9985 residual "(iii) Support services other than (i) and (ii) above": 9% CGST — [GST Gyaan, Notification 11/2017 as amended, updated 2022](https://gstgyaan.com/cgst-rate-on-support-services-heading-9985)
- Notification 15/2025-Central Tax (Rate), 17 Sep 2025, which amends the services rate notification: a text search found changes for headings such as 9988 and 9997, and no entry for 9985 — [gazette copy](https://bcasonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/No.15-2025-Central-Tax-Rate.pdf)
- Entry 3, Notification 12/2017-Central Tax (Rate): "Pure services (excluding works contract service or other composite supplies involving supply of any goods) provided to the Central Government, State Government or Union territory or local authority... by way of any activity in relation to any function entrusted to a Panchayat under article 243G... or... a Municipality under article 243W": Nil. — [official text](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-05/download_6_0.pdf). The words "or a Governmental authority or a Government Entity" were omitted from 1 Jan 2022 by Notification 16/2021 — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/pure-services-gst-detailed-analysis.html)
- Entry 3B, inserted by Notification 13/2023 from 20 Oct 2023: "Services provided to a Governmental Authority by way of - (a) water supply; (b) public health; (c) sanitation conservancy; (d) solid waste management; and (e) slum improvement and upgradation": Nil — [official text](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-05/13-2023-ctr-eng.pdf)
- Entry 66(b)(iii): services to an educational institution by way of "security or cleaning or house-keeping services performed in such educational institution", limited by proviso to pre-school and education up to higher secondary or equivalent — [official text](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-05/download_6_0.pdf); colleges excluded per an AAR — [Tax commentary](https://taxmann.com/post/blog/manpower-supply-of-housekeeping-cleaning-security-etc-to-govt-colleges-is-not-exempt-from-gst-aar)
- AARs split on Entry 3 and rulings bind only the applicant; one found housekeeping at IIITDM taxable under both 3B and 66 — search summary of [AAR compilation](https://logitax.in/resources/exemption_of_services_provided_to_government_aar_compilation)
- Tender practice: CUP, IIT Delhi and IIML require or state GST-inclusive rates; ACTREC stated its estimate excluding GST; ICMR's 2020 format said "Add GST 12%", which is unexplained — documents in section 1

### Inferences
- MC Chandigarh or Mohali water works: plausibly Nil under Entry 3 if pure service; chemicals could make it composite. Schools: plausibly Nil under 66(b)(iii), since tank cleaning is cleaning performed in the school, but no ruling on tanks was found. Central universities, IITs, AIIMS, PSUs and private hospitals: charge 18%.
- A quote "inclusive of GST" loses 15.25% of the price to tax on taxable jobs, which matters at L1. The owner's CA should sign off before an exemption is claimed on an invoice.

### Gaps
- The CBIC site was unreachable, so the consolidated 11/2017 text and official SAC annexure for 998534 were not read directly. No ruling specific to tank cleaning was found. TDS on government contracts was not researched.

## 7. Regulation that drives demand

### Takeaway
I found no statute or bye-law in Chandigarh or Punjab that mandates periodic tank cleaning, and no CPCB or PPCB rule. Demand comes from FSSAI records for food businesses, accreditation practice in hospitals, and buyers' own contract frequency, usually one to three cleans a year.

### Cited Findings
- FSSAI Schedule 4 Part II clause 4.1.3: water storage tanks cleaned periodically, with records in a register, per compliance guides — [TaxClue](https://www.taxclue.in/blog/schedule-4-part-2-fssai-licensing-regulations-location-premises-equipment-and-facilities/), [Petpooja](https://blog.petpooja.com/?p=48877); regulation text not read
- FSSAI audit sample: water testing not mandatory for operators on municipal potable water if bills are kept — [FSSAI sample checklist](https://hygiene.fssai.gov.in/resources/sample_checklist.pdf) (snippet)
- NABH: a sample document lists "cleaning of tanks" with date and next due, and says contents are "not prescribed by NABH as mandatory practices" — [NABH sample via Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1es5MPX0D9OsSzCGRsQdvi4q0Ir9HwDJF/export?format=pdf). A training deck names monthly microbiological and three-monthly biochemical water tests against IS 10500 — [BCMCH training](https://sites.google.com/view/bcmchtraining/accreditation/nabh-6th-edition/08-fms-chapter). Both are secondary
- GeM's CSD service makes healthcare spaces follow National Guidelines for Clean Hospitals 2015 — [GeM SLA](https://bidplus.gem.gov.in/bidding/bid/bidsla/10948700089924)
- Nearest government order: J&K School Education Jammu, 24 Mar 2025: overhead and underground tanks to be cleaned at six-month intervals by trained or authorised providers, records posted on the tank — [DSEJ circular](https://schedujammu.nic.in/orders_circulars/DSEJ-24-03-2025.pdf)
- Punjab: Education Department deep-cleaning instructions, with a ₹24.52 crore release, include "sanitisation of drinking water tanks and sources with chlorine/bleach, along with water quality testing". This is a one-off drive, with no interval set — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/punjab/punjab-education-department-releases-rs-24-52-crore-for-deep-cleansing-of-schools)
- Chandigarh bye-laws: Water Supply Bye-Law 2011 and 2018 amendment are listed, text not read; MC notices target overflowing tanks and wastage, not hygiene — [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/10540?locale=hi), [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/chandigarh/8-challaned-86-get-notice-for-wasting-water-239568)
- Contamination news in Chandigarh: sewage reaching tanks in CHB colonies — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/chandigarh/sewage-mixing-with-drinking-water-supply-at-chb-colonies/amp); human rights notice over dirty water in Maloya — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/chandigarh/mc-commissioner-gets-notice-over-dirty-water-supply-to-maloya)
- Six-monthly advice is common but not law: 3 to 6 months for concrete tanks, once a year for sealed plastic — [Tribune, 2019](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/archive/opt-for-a-thorough-cleaning-method-for-water-tanks-719111)

### Inferences
- Copy should say "records" and "buyer's contract schedule", not "mandated by law". Do not claim NABH, NBC or a Chandigarh rule requires six-monthly cleaning unless the source text is read.
- The strongest compliance hook for hotels and food factories is the FSSAI register; for hospitals, the NABH-style due-date tag; for government, the DSR-style dated stencil and tank register.

### Gaps
- NBC 2016 Part 9, the CPHEEO manual, NABH standards text, the Chandigarh Water Supply Bye-Law 2011, Punjab Municipal Building Bye-Laws and the Factories Act state rules were not read, as none was available in full text. A rule could exist in any of them.
- Nothing found from CPCB or PPCB on storage tank cleaning.

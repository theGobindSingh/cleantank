# National and regional brands in Indian water tank cleaning, compared with a Tricity operator

Research date: 2026-10-31. Every fact was read on that date unless another date is given.

How to read the evidence labels:
- **[live]**: I opened the page today and read it.
- **[listing]**: a franchise directory page, opened today. These pages are self-reported and undated, and they often contradict each other.
- **[founder]**: an interview or profile where the company is the only source.
- **[extract]**: seen only in a search result summary. I could not open the page, so check it before quoting.

The Clean Tank baseline for comparison is from the brief: ₹600 (to 500 L) to ₹2,000 (to 10,000 L) before 18% GST, which is ₹708 to ₹2,360 with GST (my arithmetic). Franchise is ₹3 to 8 lakh plus 3.5% royalty per month.

Method limits:
- Search tools are US-based and English-only, so they surface Hindi, Punjabi, YouTube and Justdial content poorly.
- The Playwright browser was shared with other researchers and was navigated away mid-check (my evaluate call returned a Google Maps page). Urban Company prices therefore come from fetching the page, not from clicking through it. I did not see the size options inside each service.
- MCA data was not opened. Tofler was not checked.

## Who are the organised players?

### Takeaway
Of the 14 names in the brief, only Urban Company has a confirmed branded, priced tank cleaning service in the Tricity. Sulekha is confirmed live there too, but it only passes on leads. The closest organised rival I found was not on the list: Mr. TEMS / Crystal Clean of Mohali. Pure-play tank cleaning has no funded national brand. It is a set of small franchisors, plus home-service apps that add tanks as a line item.

### Inferences
- Threat ranking for the Tricity, my judgement:
  1. Urban Company on retail price anchoring and reviews.
  2. Mr. TEMS / Crystal Clean, if still active: same region, same method story, same franchise pitch, and institutional references.

A search summary could not verify the business from that page, because it found no address or company name there. This will matter for buyers doing the same diligence.

- Urban Company's review count may be pooled nationally, so 16K is not proof of Tricity volume. Review addresses on the page do include Panchkula and Zirakpur.

### Inferences
- Royalty maths: Fastclean's flat ₹24,000 a year is ₹2,000 a month, equal to 3.5% of ₹57,143 monthly revenue. Above that revenue Fastclean's unit pays less than Clean Tank's. At an illustrative ₹2 lakh a month, a 3.5% royalty is ₹7,000 against ₹20,000 at 10%.
- If "3.5% per month" means 3.5% of monthly revenue, the brochure should say so. Read literally against investment, it could be misread. Confirm the wording.
- The top of Clean Tank's range (₹8 lakh) exceeds every pure-play listing's upper bound except the Glowtank ₹10 lakh variant. The low royalty is the honest counter-argument, along with equipment if the kit is real. Mr. TEMS's ₹8 lakh machine figure and Fastclean's van and equipment split ([₹4 to 6 lakh, including ₹1 to 1.5 lakh licence](https://www.franchiseindia.com/insights/en/article/fastclean-a-hygienic-franchise-initiative.35723), 2017) give useful reference points.
- Fastclean reported 40+ franchisees in 2014, 48+ in 2017, and 41 to 45+ now. If those are comparable, the largest pure-play network has been flat for over a decade. That points to high churn or weak unit economics, so a franchisor making conservative, documented claims would stand out. Treat this as a hypothesis.
- No competitor discloses territory size. A defined pin-code or population territory would be a real selling point.

### Gaps

- Franchisee interviews, churn and any actual Tricity unit for TANKRO, Fastclean or Easy Tank Clean.
- Listing dates. Most directory pages show only a 2026 footer, so terms may be years old.

## Industry standards, training and emerging technology

### Takeaway
I found no Indian standard, licence or training credential specific to cleaning drinking water tanks. WHO provides the only concrete procedure I could read. "Robotic" tank cleaning for homes remains a marketing word: the actual robots are for septic and oil tanks.

### Cited Findings

**WHO**

- [WHO Technical Note 3, Cleaning and disinfecting water storage tanks and tankers](https://inspectapedia.com/plumbing/Water-Storage-Tank-Ganker-Cleaning-WHO.pdf) sets out four steps: select tanks, clean, disinfect, safely dispose. Dose: fill a quarter, add 80 g of high-strength calcium hypochlorite per 1,000 L capacity, fill, close and stand 24 hours. Doubling the dose cuts this to 8 hours. It was written for emergencies, not routine domestic service [live, PDF text read].
- [WHO and World Plumbing Council, Health Aspects of Plumbing (2006)](https://bdd.pseau.org/outils/ouvrages/oms_health_aspects_of_plumbing_2006.pdf), section 12.2: tanks should have a close-fitting lid and be "inspected periodically and disinfected". No interval is given. New installations get 20 to 50 mg/L free chlorine, standing one to two hours. It also says fixtures should be connected directly, avoiding a tank, where supply is continuous at positive pressure [live, PDF text read].

**India**

- BIS: [IS 2065:1983, water supply in buildings](https://www.services.bis.gov.in/tmp/SR2065.pdf), is the governing code, and [IS 12183](https://services.bis.gov.in/tmp/SR12183_1.pdf) carries its maintenance rules to multi-storey buildings [extract]. National Building Code Part 9 covers plumbing. I could not read any tank cleaning clause or interval in these.
- CPHEEO: the [Manual on Water Supply and Treatment](https://pt.ircwash.org/sites/default/files/254.2-10207.pdf) was seen only as a bibliographic reference [extract]. Its text was not obtained.
- No Indian rule mandates a cleaning interval that I could find. A [Jammu school education order of March 2025](https://schedujammu.nic.in/orders_circulars/DSEJ-24-03-2025.pdf) requires six-monthly cleaning in schools [extract]. Mandatory intervals exist abroad: [Abu Dhabi, six months](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/abu-dhabi-drinking-water-tanks-cleaning-all-you-need-to-know) [extract] and [Japan, yearly for tanks over 10 m³](https://www.niph.go.jp/soshiki/suido/pdf/h19JPUS/abstract/r07.pdf) [extract].
- The [Manual Scavengers Act 2013](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/8868) bans hazardous cleaning of sewers and septic tanks. Search found nothing extending it to drinking water tanks [extract].

**Training and accreditation**

- No ISSA, CIMS or IICRC tank cleaning credential in India was found, from one search.
- [IAPMO India's Certified Service Technician](https://iapmo.org/media/20bhsgkw/certified_service_technician_programme_brochure.pdf) covers residential water treatment, not tanks [extract].
- Training that exists is in-house: Mr. TEMS's [ShubhArambh](https://www.siliconindia.com/mr-dot-tems), and [3 days to 1 week at Glowtank](https://franchisebazar.com/franchise-opportunity/glowtank).

**Technology**

- Mechanised dewatering, pressure jet, vacuum and UV is the standard kit across brands (see above).
- [GD Water Consult](https://gdwaterconsult.com/tank-cleaning-business-franchise) mentions "robotic cleaning systems" in a blog post, without specifics [live]. [mytankclean](https://mytankcleancom1.odoo.com) claims cleaning without human entry in [30 minutes](https://mytankcleancom1.odoo.com/about-us) [extract, page now 404].
- [Gridbots' SaUsR](https://www.electronicsforu.com/electronics-startups/innovations-innovators/underwater-robot-clean-tanks) (15 June 2012) cleaned full tanks, cutting water waste to 5 to 10%. It was leased at ₹15 lakh a year. I found no evidence of a consumer service.
- Adjacent robots: [Homosep, septic tanks](https://dst.gov.in/indias-1st-septic-tank-cleaning-robot-strengthening-swatch-bharat-campaign) [extract] and [BetaTank, petrochemical tanks](https://frontiertech.niti.gov.in/story/from-suction-to-pump-how-betatank-robotics-redesigned-tank-cleaning-technology/) [extract].
- A consumer in-tank cleaning device sold without draining or entry is [listed on Moglix](https://www.moglix.com/hanbaz-vortex-16m-abs-extra-large-edition-blue-water-tank-cleaner/mp/msne5n8p1wvykl) [extract]. This is a do-it-yourself substitute.
- No drone tank cleaning for drinking water was found.

### Inferences
- With no standard to meet, credibility has to be built. Publishing a method tied to WHO dosing and a measured chlorine residual, with a signed report, would be stronger than any "6-stage" claim, because every rival claims stages and few show measurements.
- WHO's dose is for emergency rehabilitation. It should inform a procedure, and a qualified person should set the routine one.
- Marketing ranges from "6-stage" to "German" to "100% bacteria free" with no evidence. Clean Tank should avoid copying absolute claims, partly for accuracy and partly because they invite challenge.
- Water saving (Chandigarh penalises waste) is the most credible future angle for no-drain methods. There is no evidence yet that the kit is affordable for a ₹600 job.

### Gaps
- Exact text of IS 2065, NBC Part 9 and the CPHEEO manual on tank cleaning. These need the source documents.
- Whether any Punjab, Haryana or Chandigarh health or municipal rule sets an interval.
- ISSA and IICRC were checked only through a search, not on their own sites.

## Market size, demand and seasonality in North India

### Takeaway
No credible market size exists for tank cleaning services in India. Demand signals are real but indirect, and Tricity tanks are not going away soon because the Chandigarh 24x7 project has stalled. I found no measured seasonality. The six-month, pre- and post-monsoon rhythm is advice from the trade, not data.

### Cited Findings

**Market reports (all LOW CONFIDENCE, report mill or adjacent category)**

- India water storage tank sales, not cleaning: USD 965.1 million in 2023, about USD 1,364.7 million by 2030 at 5.2% CAGR, attributed to Report Ocean. Syndicated press release, seen only in a search summary ([IPS News](https://ipsnews.net/business/?p=108783)) [extract]. LOW CONFIDENCE.
- India water purifier services, a different market: [USD 511 million in 2026 to USD 877 million by 2032, MarkNtel](https://www.marknteladvisors.com/research-library/india-water-purifier-service-market-report.html) [extract]. LOW CONFIDENCE.
- [Global tank cleaning services report](https://www.strategicrevenueinsights.com/industry/tank-cleaning-service-market) has no India figure seen. [IMARC India cleaning services](https://www.imarcgroup.com/india-cleaning-services-market/toc) has no figure seen.
- GD Water Consult's ["The Lucrative Future of Tank Cleaning"](https://gdwaterconsult.com/future-tank-cleaning-business-india.aspx) (4 Nov 2023) has only unquantified claims and comes from a franchise seller [live].

**Demand signals**

- Urban Company's Chandigarh page shows [about 19K reviews across three tank items](https://www.urbancompany.com/chandigarh-plumbers) [live]. Possibly pooled across cities.
- Tank cleaning is bought by institutions: a tender for [overhead PVC tanks and an underground reservoir at the Punjab and Haryana High Court](https://tender18.com/tenders-details/15948422) [extract], plus Air Force and CPWD sites. Elsewhere, AIIMS Raipur [specifies a mechanised process](https://website.aiimsraipur.edu.in/Quotation/NIQ-17%20WATER%20TANK%20CLEANINg,Hospital.pdf) [extract].
- Mr. TEMS cites [UltraTech, Trident, Punjab University, Bestech and a Bathinda cantonment with about 10,000 tanks](https://www.siliconindia.com/mr-dot-tems) [founder].
- Health trigger: [cholera in Peermuchalla, Zirakpur, 16 Sept 2021](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/chandigarh/7-of-10-water-samples-from-peermuchalla-fail-test-311896), with 282 cases and 7 of 10 samples failing. The council planned to chlorinate its own and private tanks. Officials blamed individual submersible pumps, so tanks were not the established cause [live].
- Chandigarh structural demand: the pan-city 24x7 project shows [no significant progress, cost about ₹1,500 crore, target 2027 (2 Oct 2025)](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/chandigarh/pan-city-24x7-water-supply-project-in-doldrums-as-cost-triples-in-3-yrs) [live]. A [Manimajra pilot was commissioned in August 2024](https://indianinfrastructure.com/?p=188824) [extract]; the Tribune reports criticism of its results.
- Chandigarh [restricted morning-supply use from 10 April to 30 June 2026 and treats tank overflow as a violation](https://thevoiceofchandigarh.com/?p=67259) [extract].

**Frequency and seasonality**

- [Every six months, timed to pre- and post-monsoon](https://www.studiomatrx.org/guides/water-tank-cleaning-india), per one guide [extract]. [Twice a year per MannuBhai](https://www.mannubhai.com/margaon%20/tank-cleaning-service) [extract].
- Fastclean said [customers return after four to six months](https://www.franchiseindia.com/insights/en/article/fastclean-a-hygienic-franchise-initiative.35723) (2017) [founder].
- KPS offers [quarterly or half-yearly AMC visits](https://kps.co.in/water-tank-cleaning-amc/chandigarh/panchkula) [live].

### Inferences
- Any market number in the report must be built bottom-up (households with tanks × cleans a year × price). The notes above have no sourced input for that, so don't quote a market size.
- Likely peaks are April to June (summer and pre-monsoon) and September to November (post-monsoon, after contamination news). This is reasoned from the trade's six-month advice and local events, not measured. Clean Tank's own booking history would settle it and be better than any public source.
- WHO's preference for direct connection under continuous pressure means full 24x7 supply is a long-term risk to home demand. In Chandigarh it looks years off on the evidence above. Societies and institutions would still store water.
- Six-monthly reminders and AMC capture the repeat cycle, and only KPS publishes AMC pricing.

### Gaps
- Number of households or buildings with tanks in the Tricity, and the share using professional cleaners. Census housing data was not checked.
- Any monthly demand data. Google Trends was not available through these tools.
- Mohali and Panchkula water supply status. Searches returned nothing recent for them.
- Waterborne illness data for 2025 and 2026 in the Tricity. Search found none.

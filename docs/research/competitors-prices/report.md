# Clean Tank prices sit mid-market, reviews lag

Roughly 70 local businesses clean water tanks in the Chandigarh Tricity, from about 90 names found, and no single firm leads every channel: Welco leads Google Maps, A-One leads Justdial, Busy Bucket leads organic search among real businesses, Urban Company leads online booking, and Good Life Future leads Meta ads. Published home prices run from ₹399 and ₹499 teasers to Guga Aviation's ₹4,999 for 10,000 L, and a 1,000 L overhead tank usually lists at ₹779 to ₹1,499. With GST, Clean Tank's ₹944 for that tank is almost exactly Urban Company's ₹949, its 2,000 L sump at ₹1,180 is ₹519 under Urban Company, and its list stays above the ₹399 to ₹500 local floor at every size. Against metro rate cards it runs high on 5,000 L overhead tanks and on sumps of 15,000 L and up, but those cards do not serve the Tricity. Nobody publishes a real Tricity society rate, and the one Tricity government award found went to a general contractor at 18.9% under estimate. Clean Tank's largest gap is visibility: it appeared in no search sample or Maps list tested, and that test covered the old site. Its list also has one clear fault, because a 30,001 L tank costs ₹2,100 less than a 30,000 L tank. All of this rests on public listings read on 31 October 2026 from a Mumbai server. Nobody was phoned, so these are listed prices, not paid ones.

## Clean Tank matches Urban Company at 1,000 L and undercuts it on small sumps

Homes buy per tank by capacity band everywhere. All three sized cards that reach the Tricity also price by tank type, while Clean Tank charges by size alone. Local sized cards are rare: Urban Company, Guga and HomeTriangle's template are the only three, and everything else is a "from" price. Those floors are ₹399 for 500 to 2,000 L in a [20 October Meta ad](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1407923478165926), ₹499 from [Grace](https://gracecleaning.in/water-tank-cleaning/) and Fixomes, and ₹500 from [ServiceSutra](https://www.servicesutra.com/chandigarh/water-tank-cleaning). [Favorz](https://www.favorz.co.in/commercial-cleaning/water-tank-cleaning-service-chandigarh), a Gurgaon firm with 10 ratings, shows ₹245. None gives the size behind the price, except that ad.

Homes pay one figure, so the fair column is Clean Tank with GST, against rivals' listed prices. Almost no rival page says whether its price includes tax. The medians come from a dozen city and national cards, use range midpoints, treat "from" prices as the price, and rest on thin samples above 2,000 L. At 10,000 L overhead there are only three cards. **Clean Tank is 29% to 53% under Guga at every overhead size even with GST**, though Guga's price bundles water testing and a certificate. **Against the national median, the 500 L and 1,000 L bands are 14% under and 3% over before GST, then 1% and 21% over with it.** The list is strongest on small sumps and on RCC. Only HomeTriangle prices RCC overhead tanks, at ₹1,199 to ₹2,949, and Clean Tank is 28% to 33% under that before GST and 15% to 21% under it with GST. The 5,000 L overhead band is the weakest spot on the card, at 47% over the median with GST, though only 10% over HomeTriangle. Above 10,000 L the benchmark decides the verdict: Clean Tank is about double the metro cards, which look too cheap to be full jobs, and near or below the only cards that even claim to reach the Tricity. A 5,000 L sump also needs care, because ₹1,652 is below the median yet 14% above HomeTriangle's ₹1,449.

City size barely moves these numbers. Bengaluru, Mumbai and Pune cards are often below Tricity ones, and Urban Company's own range runs only from [₹799 in Lucknow](https://www.urbancompany.com/lucknow-plumbers) to ₹949 here and in [Jaipur](https://www.urbancompany.com/jaipur-plumbers). Urban Company also moves: search copies of its Chandigarh page showed ₹799 and ₹1,099 for overhead, so re-check before leaning on ₹949.

GST is Clean Tank's quiet handicap. Registration starts at [₹20 lakh turnover for service providers](https://cleartax.in/s/gst-registration-limits-increased), about 2,500 jobs a year at ₹800, so small operators probably need not charge it, though no data shows how many do. A rival's ₹800 is likely the final price, while Clean Tank's becomes ₹944. The flip side is that a [proper GST bill is already an advertised benefit](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=26586607767617468) here.

Multi-tank pricing is mixed. Two 1,000 L tanks cost ₹1,699 with GST after the 10% discount, against ₹1,099 to ₹1,299 from [KaamGenie in Gurgaon](https://kaamgenie.com/blog/water-tank-cleaning-cost-gurgaon). A 1,000 L overhead tank plus a 5,000 L sump costs ₹2,336 with GST, inside [KaamGenie Delhi's ₹2,200 to ₹3,000](https://kaamgenie.com/delhi/water-tank-cleaning) combined visit.

The list has one clear fault. Because ₹0.13/L applies to the whole tank, 30,000 L costs ₹6,000 and 30,001 L costs ₹3,900. A 35,000 L tank at ₹4,550 costs less than a 25,000 L tank at ₹5,000, and the price only returns to ₹6,000 at about 46,154 L. Charging ₹0.13/L only on the litres above 30,000 would remove the drop: 50,000 L would be ₹8,600 instead of ₹6,500. A ₹6,000 floor would work too. Few homes are affected, but a calculator will show the drop to anyone who tries it.

## No Tricity society or private AMC rate is public

Societies, hotels and hospitals buy on private quotes, so this segment has the weakest evidence. The only AMC bands anywhere are [KPS's indicative yearly figures](https://kps.co.in/water-tank-cleaning-amc/chandigarh/panchkula): ₹6,000 to ₹15,000 for an individual building, ₹15,000 to ₹50,000 for a society, and ₹30,000 to ₹1,00,000 for commercial sites. The same bands appear on its [Gurgaon](https://kps.co.in/water-tank-cleaning-amc/gurgaon) and [Pune](https://kps.co.in/water-tank-cleaning-amc/pune/wakad) pages, so they are templates and not local evidence. Search tools still repeated them as a benchmark, which shows what a published number can do.

Other signals are scattered and unverified: [₹1/L for societies and ₹0.20/L for cement tanks](https://dir.indiamart.com/mohali/water-tank-cleaning-services.html) on IndiaMART snippets for Mohali, and [₹0.30 to ₹0.40/L](https://www.arwatertankcleaners.in/water-tank-cleaning-price/) with a ₹4,000 minimum above 10,000 L from a Delhi vendor guide. Discounts of [15% to 25%](https://kaamgenie.com/delhi/water-tank-cleaning) and [20% to 30%](https://loklfix.in/blog/water-tank-cleaning-bangalore-guide.html) for society AMCs appear only in other cities.

As a worked example of mine, a society with two 1,00,000 L tanks cleaned twice a year would pay ₹52,000 before GST at the home ₹0.13/L, or ₹61,360 with GST, which is just above KPS's society band. The home list does not formally apply to societies, but its top rate is about where a real society negotiation starts. A Mohali society AMC query surfaced no Mohali-based AMC page, so this segment looks open. Crystal Clean's institutional story and KPS's pages are its nearest occupants.

## Government rates fall steeply with size, and L1 sets the price

Size drives unit price more than sector does. The Central rate book gives [₹0.40/L for terrace tanks to 2,000 L and ₹71.30 per sqm of inside surface for sumps and RCC tanks](https://cpmgsupune.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/DSR2021-Vol-II.pdf) in 2021, and those rates already carry GST. The [2023 sqm rate was corrected to ₹84.85](https://nsrcivil.in/cpwd-dsr-2023-corrections/). The best regional benchmark is the [Central University of Punjab's July 2026 estimate](https://cup.edu.in/sites/default/files/NITWTC.pdf): ₹82.55 per sqm, ₹0.48/L up to 2,000 L and ₹0.35/L above it, ₹5,11,851 in all. These are buyers' estimates, so winning bids land lower.

The sqm rows are my arithmetic on plain cuboid tanks, and a [real 4.5 lakh L reservoir at MAKAUT](https://www.makautwb.ac.in/datas/users/0-tender_undergroundtank25.pdf) fits them at ₹41,464. Since rate-book figures include GST, the like-for-like home rate above 30,000 L is ₹0.153/L, not ₹0.13. Whole-campus estimates blend tank sizes: [₹0.51/L](https://home.iitd.ac.in/public/storage/tenders/NIT_1792576541.pdf) and [₹0.42/L](https://home.iitd.ac.in/public/storage/tenders_archives/NIT_1789386390.pdf) at IIT Delhi, inclusive of GST, and about [₹0.057/L at IIM Lucknow](https://www.iiml.ac.in/sites/default/files/upload/tender/1817788013Revised.pdf). Clean Tank's own IIM Lucknow certificates sit near that estimate, which suggests its bulk billing already runs far under the home list. That rests on an assumed scope match and needs the owner's check. Those contract values are internal and must stay off the site.

Tricity tenders rarely state capacity, so they give values and no per-litre rates. The [CCET Sector 26 job](https://www.tendershark.com/details/chandigarh-tender/administration/3292226b-dd9b-47f4-8242-d44ef3b442d1) was estimated at ₹3,25,201 and went to L1 M/s Dinesh Pandey at ₹2,63,738; an elevator contractor bid L2. That is the only Tricity award found, so "who wins" rests on one case. Other live or recent values are [₹2,65,274 at Tata Memorial HBCHRC, New Chandigarh](https://www.tenderdetail.com/State-tenders/punjab-tenders/cleaning-of-water-tank-tenders) (snippet only) and [₹3,83,522 for MC Chandigarh's Jandpur raw water tank](https://www.tenderdetail.com/Indian-Tenders/TenderNotice/58245353/23ea1004201e3eab6379b3bfb4d7c33f). Tank work also hides inside bigger jobs, such as [a ₹35 lakh MES contract at 3 BRD](https://www.tenderdetail.com/Indian-Tenders/TenderNotice/57980197/79773eaba8d72bf591fd27333df179d9). No tender surfaced for PGIMER, GMCH-32, Panjab University, PEC, IISER Mohali or ISB, which means only that none was indexed.

The threat is price, more than qualification. The recurring gates are GST, PAN, ESI and EPF, similar-work value, and three years of accounts, and [IIT Delhi](https://home.iitd.ac.in/public/storage/tenders/NIT_1792576541.pdf) also wants CPWD, MES, BSNL or Railways registration. Clean Tank holds GST, EPFO, ESI, MSME and BSNL enlistment, and looks able to clear these on most Tricity-sized jobs, subject to each tender's class and value rules. No document searched asked for ISO. Bids must carry testing, disposal and usually GST inside the rate, and an inclusive quote gives up 15.25% to tax on taxable work. [Municipal-function](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-05/download_6_0.pdf) and [water supply or sanitation work for a Governmental Authority](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-05/13-2023-ctr-eng.pdf), and cleaning in schools up to higher secondary, may be Nil-rated, but rulings are split and a CA should sign off first.

## Clean Tank's openings fit its rules, with one conflict

Clean Tank Services has worked since 2008, but search does not yet show it here. [cleantankservices.com](https://www.cleantankservices.com/) appeared in no clean organic sample, a by-name Maps search returned [only other firms](https://www.google.com/maps/search/Clean%20Tank%20Services%20Kharar), and no Justdial page turned up. That test covered the old site, whose homepage never mentions the Tricity, and it ran from Mumbai, so someone should repeat it on a phone in Mohali. Two checks fall to the owner: whether a Google Business Profile or Justdial listing exists, and why the registry shows the domain [created on 16 September 2026](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/cleantankservices.com) despite [captures since 2018](https://web.archive.org/web/20241226115335/https://cleantankservices.com/). That date suggests the name lapsed and was re-registered, which could have cost old links. Search Console will show it. Domain age is not fatal, since 2024 to 2026 domains already reach page one.

The new site's clearest opening is language. **No Devanagari or Gurmukhi appeared on any of 14 competitor sites tested**, one page each, and a Hindi Maps query returned the same English-named firms. Hindi selling exists only in [Meta ads](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1407923478165926), and Punjabi results were [nothing local](https://fina-music.com/page/2). Price is the second opening, because few pages answer "how much" and the ["mohali price" results](https://www.justdial.com/Mohali/Water-Tank-Cleaning-Services-for-Manual-Cleaning-in-Mohali-Sector-91/nct-10532) were directories, Facebook, Amazon and OLX. The calculator should show the with-GST total as the headline. Thin coverage in Kharar, New Chandigarh and Dera Bassi is the third, though Wazara, A-One, Safai Guru and Welco's ads are active around Clean Tank's Kharar office. Kurali and Ropar gave no valid data.

Proof is the strongest available message. Stage counts of four to eight and UV are everywhere, so neither sets anyone apart. Among sites read, only Clean Tank gives an ISO certificate number; use the approved wording and never "accredited". A written report helps too: [Guga](https://gugaaviation.com/services/water-tank-cleaning/chandigarh/) and [KPS](https://kps.co.in/single-tank-cleaning/chandigarh) describe certificates, but none of it was independently verified. Reviews are the real shortfall, with leaders holding hundreds and Clean Tank none found. [Welco's public reviews](https://business.google.com/v/welco-tank-cleaning-services/05022997511415029701/7d5e/_/rev) praise punctuality, polite staff and seeing the tank washed from inside, a small sample that hints at what customers fear. Complaints themselves could not be read.

Three lines stay out of bounds. Good Life Future's ["not local laborers"](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=26586607767617468) breaks the rule on plumbers and labourers. Rivals' ["99.99%"](https://gugaaviation.com/services/water-tank-cleaning/mohali/), "WHO-approved" and ["100% bacteria-free"](https://busybucket.in/water-tank-cleaning-in-chandigarh/) cannot be verified. And nothing should name a rival or print a price comparison, since a plain published price makes the point alone. Compliance copy needs care too: no Chandigarh or Punjab rule mandating periodic cleaning was found. The nearest was [a Mohali order during the 2024 cholera outbreak](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/chandigarh/amid-cholera-outbreak-in-mohalis-kumbhra-fir-registered-against-2-pg-owners-for-violating-orders-643871), whose current status is unchecked.

One recommendation needs the owner's decision. Publishing an AMC price would fill a real gap, but`docs/PRODUCT.md` says organisations get no public price list. The middle path is a published AMC structure, with visit frequency, paperwork and what the rate includes, while the rupees stay on quote.

## Conclusion

At the sizes most homes buy, Clean Tank's prices already hold up. Its two problems are the 18% GST that a smaller rival may not charge and its absence from every place a Tricity buyer currently looks. Price cuts would only chase a ₹399 teaser, whereas a Google Business Profile, a Justdial listing and genuine reviews work on the channels where firms with no website are winning. If the owner ever wants more margin, sumps and RCC tanks are where the market pays above the list, though charging for them would cost the simplicity of pricing by size alone.

The home list also has a limit in the other segments. At 5 lakh L its ₹0.13/L is about double the rate-book figure, so it cannot double as an institutional rate. Government work will be priced against rate books and L1 contractors, and society work is open to whoever defines the terms first. The next step costs little: about 10 phone calls for quotes in Mohali, Panchkula and Kharar, for one tank size, would give real local rates, including the plumber's price that no public source records.

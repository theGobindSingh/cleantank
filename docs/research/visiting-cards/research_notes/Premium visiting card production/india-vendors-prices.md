# Premium visiting card production in India: vendor capabilities

Scope note: the user changed the brief partway through. All price research was dropped, so these notes contain no INR prices, setup charges or price comparisons. The price questions are not answered here on purpose. They cover what vendors can make, whether per-person staff runs are feasible, and local options. Buyer is in Kharar, Punjab. Pages were checked around 1 November 2026. Research stopped early, so "not found" below means not found in a short search, not proof that a vendor does not exist.

## Which online vendors serve premium cards in India, and what can they make?

### Takeaway
Vistaprint India and Printo are confirmed, with product pages showing velvet or soft-touch stock at 350 GSM and foil options. PrintStop is confirmed as active, but its capabilities come only from its own blog. Printland, Inkmonk, Zoomin, Printasia and MOO's India delivery could not be confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Vistaprint India Velvet Touch cards use 350 GSM stock with a double-sided soft-touch coating, in standard, rounded-corner and square formats — [Vistaprint India](https://www.vistaprint.in/business-cards/velvet-touch-visiting-cards)
- Vistaprint India's card range also lists Glossy, Matte, Non-Tearable, Spot UV, Raised Foil, Premium Plus Glossy, Magnetic and Transparent, plus standard, classic, rounded, square, leaf, oval, circle and custom shapes — [Vistaprint India](https://www.vistaprint.in/business-cards)
- Vistaprint India's product results also show 350 GSM Premium Matte and Premium Glossy; this came from a search summary and I did not open those pages — [Vistaprint India matte](https://www.vistaprint.in/business-cards/matte-visiting-cards); [Vistaprint India glossy](https://www.vistaprint.in/business-cards/glossy-visiting-cards)
- Printo Velvet Touch cards come as Spot UV, Foiled, or Spot UV + Foiled, on 300 GSM coated paper (Lykam) or 350 GSM coated paper (Ninbo Star), with gold or silver foil. Velvet lamination is the feature, and matte lamination is also listed — [Printo](https://printo.in/categories/premiumfinish-business-cards/customizable-products/velvet-touch-business-cards)
- Printo applies Spot UV and foil to one side of the card only, and the Velvet Touch minimum is 100 cards — [Printo](https://printo.in/categories/premiumfinish-business-cards/customizable-products/velvet-touch-business-cards)
- Printo's Chennai page says custom cards generally start from 50 and lists standard, matte, glossy, textured, premium, transparent, metallic and luxury types — [Printo Chennai](https://printo.in/p/visiting-card-printing-in-chennai-premium-cards)
- Printo advertises 4-hour orders in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, NCR and Chennai. The banner does not say this covers velvet cards, and Punjab is not listed — [Printo](https://printo.in/categories/premiumfinish-business-cards/customizable-products/velvet-touch-business-cards)
- PrintStop describes its range as 300 to 400+ GSM with matte, gloss, Spot UV, foil, linen-textured, PVC and eco stocks, and nationwide delivery. This is the vendor's own marketing, updated June 2026 — [PrintStop blog](https://www.printstop.co.in/blog/best-visiting-card-printing-companies-india)
- The same PrintStop blog lists other vendors: Printlipi (300 to 400 GSM, matte, textured), Quapri (300 to 350 GSM, matte, UV), Vivid Print (300 to 400 GSM, gloss, textured), ARC Print (300 to 450 GSM, "premium finishes"), Swadesh Graphics (280 to 350 GSM, matte, regional delivery), Printdrop and EprintCafe (300 GSM). A competitor wrote these and they were not checked against each vendor — [PrintStop blog](https://www.printstop.co.in/blog/best-visiting-card-printing-companies-india)
- That blog understates its rivals: it gives Vistaprint and Printo as matte and gloss only, while their own pages show velvet and foil — [PrintStop blog](https://www.printstop.co.in/blog/best-visiting-card-printing-companies-india); contradicted by [Vistaprint India](https://www.vistaprint.in/business-cards) and [Printo](https://printo.in/categories/premiumfinish-business-cards/customizable-products/velvet-touch-business-cards)
- MOO sells a Cotton card made from recycled cotton T-shirts — [MOO](https://moo.com/eu/business-cards/moo-size)
- SA Creative's page lists 300 GSM matte, 400 GSM soft-touch and 600 GSM cotton or textured tiers, with spot UV, foil, letterpress and edge painting. It names no registered entity and is hosted on a lovable.app subdomain, so treat it as low confidence — [SA Creative](https://sacreativestore.lovable.app/visiting-card-printing)

### Inferences
- The online vendors confirmed here top out around 350 to 450 GSM, and I found no mainstream online vendor confirming 600 GSM cotton. That stock looks like specialist work.
- Printo's foil is limited to one side, so a design with foil on both faces would need another vendor.

### Gaps
- Printland: the page failed to load and search returned nothing from the vendor. Unconfirmed.
- Inkmonk, Zoomin, Printasia: no vendor pages surfaced. Unconfirmed. Zoomin and Printasia were not searched individually.
- MOO to India: whether it ships here could not be confirmed. Its shipping help page returned 404 and the only India comments found were old forum anecdotes.
- Duplex coloured-core cards: no vendor in this list confirmed offering them.

Only Gravitfy Studio (Delhi) was confirmed with a current page offering letterpress on cotton. Chase the Key (Bangalore) and Famous Letterpress (Nagaland) are real studios, but I did not confirm they take business-card orders today. None of the names suggested in the brief could be verified.

### Cited Findings
- Gravitfy Studio, Delhi, offers letterpress on 400 to 600 GSM cotton paper, minimum 100 pieces, 10 to 14 working days, pan-India delivery. The page carries a 2026 footer, but its share links reference an unrelated product, so confirm by phone — [Gravitfy Studio](https://gravitfystudio.com/product/letterpress-printing-gravitfy-studio-delhi/)
- Chase the Key Letterpress Studio is a Bangalore letterpress studio founded by architects and designers Arun and Akila, listed for an event at the Bangalore International Centre — [BIC](https://bangaloreinternationalcentre.org/?p=58486)
- A separate profile names the same pair's Bangalore studio as Printing with Types — [Sessionize](https://sessionize.com/arunkumar-b)
- Famous Letterpress in Dimapur, Nagaland is a design and print studio whose founder says it makes visiting cards, among other products, by letterpress — [Morung Express](https://morungexpress.com/fusion-store-welcomes-3-aesthetic-brands); [PrintWeek India](https://www.printweek.in/features/printpack-2019-visitors-speak-akanito-assumi-famous-letterpress-india-41155?amp=1)

The coverage appears dated, and the Ahmedabad location comes from a search summary — [PrintWeek India](https://www.printweek.in/news/invitations-design-studio-adopt-innovative-approach-wedding-cards-printing-24541)

### Inferences
- Wedding stationery is the main home of letterpress and foil craft in India, so wedding studios are the likeliest extra leads for premium cards.

### Gaps

## Are plates, dies and foil blocks re-usable when staff names are added?

### Takeaway
A block can be reused for the same artwork, and brass lasts longer than magnesium. No Indian source was found on whether printers hand blocks over or keep them for repeat orders. Charges are out of scope.

### Cited Findings
- Magnesium dies wear down and suit short runs. Brass dies last longer and can be reused repeatedly — [VSP Creative](https://vsp-creative.squarespace.com/which-die-do-you-need)
- Indian suppliers list magnesium foiling blocks, brass dies, and zinc and polymer embossing blocks, so all these materials can be sourced here — [IndiaMART hot stamping](https://m.indiamart.com/impcat/hot-stamping.html); [A1 Blocks](https://m.indiamart.com/a1-blocks); [Quality Block House](https://m.indiamart.com/quality-block-house/profile.html)

### Inferences
- A logo block should carry over to new staff, because the logo artwork does not change. A block holding a name cannot.

### Gaps
- Who keeps the block between orders, and for how long, is not documented. Ask each printer.

## Is a per-person run (5 to 10 names, 100 cards each) feasible with a shared logo plate and digital names?

### Takeaway
Probably feasible but unconfirmed. Online vendors can handle it as separate orders, though I found no Indian vendor that publishes a shared-plate workflow, so the hybrid needs a direct conversation with a printer.

### Cited Findings
- A 100-card minimum at Printo and Gravitfy matches a run of 100 per name, so each person can be one order at the minimum — [Printo](https://printo.in/categories/premiumfinish-business-cards/customizable-products/velvet-touch-business-cards); [Gravitfy Studio](https://gravitfystudio.com/product/letterpress-printing-gravitfy-studio-delhi/)
- Vistaprint India sells its range at 100-unit quantities and accepts uploaded designs — [Vistaprint India](https://www.vistaprint.in/business-cards/velvet-touch-visiting-cards)
- PrintStop says it offers internal brandstores for employee-level ordering, with central billing and approval steps. This is vendor marketing — [PrintStop blog](https://www.printstop.co.in/blog/best-visiting-card-printing-companies-india)

### Inferences
- The simplest route is one uploaded file per person at an online vendor, with no plate at all, if digital foil or velvet is acceptable.
- A true hybrid means foiling or pressing the logo across a batch of cards, then printing names digitally. Order matters: the stock must go through a digital press after foiling, and heavy cotton or duplex board may not feed through one. This is reasoning, not a sourced fact.
- Printing every name by letterpress would need a separate plate per name, which works against the shared-plate idea.

### Gaps
- No Indian source describes this workflow directly, and no printer was contacted.
- Whether digital presses at these vendors accept 600 GSM cotton or duplex board is unknown.

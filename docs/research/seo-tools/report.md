# Build free first, buy data last

> Correction, 31 Oct 2026: this report treats the site as new. `www.cleantankservices.com` already serves the old site, so launch is a site move with URL changes, and Search Console and Bing can start now. Track Suru (CT-257) has the move. See the `docs/AEO-GEO.md` corrections in CT-270.

Clean Tank can run a serious SEO, AEO and GEO setup for ₹0 in software plus one optional ₹4,750 DataForSEO top-up that never expires. The free core is Search Console, Keyword Planner, Bing Webmaster Tools, a Google Business Profile, Screaming Frog, PostHog and live Google autocomplete, with SiteOne or squirrelscan for scripted audits and crawlseo for ongoing monitoring on the VPS. OpenSEO deserves your good opinion, but it is a workflow layer over DataForSEO with no index of its own, so its value starts when you pay for data, and that should wait until after launch. Your view of rankme.fast holds up: it is a five-week-old, one-person beta that calls the same vendors with more containers. The bigger finding is that tools are not the constraint here. The best local evidence points to Justdial and the Business Profile as the listings that decide who gets named in Indian local answers, and neither requires any tool at all. Paid AI visibility trackers would measure a flat zero for a business with no off-site footprint, and Claude skills are checklists whose value comes from the data behind them. Everything below was checked on 31 October 2026. Nothing was installed or tested hands-on, Reddit could not be read, and most comparisons come from vendors with a stake, so prices and quality claims need a second look before you pay.

## Google and Bing cover launch for ₹0, with three catches

First-party tools are free, and for a site this small no API quota will ever bind: Search Console allows 1,200 Search Analytics queries a minute and 2,000 URL inspections a day ([Google](https://developers.google.com/webmaster-tools/limits)). Set Search Console up as a domain property from day one, because the branded queries filter works only on domain properties ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-search-console-branded-queries-filter-expands-471387)). Skip the BigQuery export. It does not reveal anonymized queries ([Search Console Help](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/12918484?hl=en)) and sandbox tables expire after 60 days ([Google Cloud](https://docs.cloud.google.com/bigquery/docs/sandbox)).

The first catch is Keyword Planner, the tool you already like. It needs billing details on file ([Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7337243)), and low-spend accounts can see buckets such as 100 to 1K instead of exact numbers. Google has never said what counts as low spend ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-officially-throttling-keyword-planner-data-low-spending-adwords-accounts-255795)), so the "$100 a month" figures on blogs have no source behind them. Only a live test on your account will settle it. The upside is targeting that no third-party tool matches: Hindi is language 1023 and Punjabi 1110 ([Google](https://developers.google.com/static/google-ads/api/data/tables/languagecodes.csv)), and the geo file has Chandigarh 1007801, Mohali sub-district 9181709, Panchkula 9299157, Kharar 9299196 and Punjab 20466, though no entry named Zirakpur ([Google](https://developers.google.com/static/google-ads/api/data/geo/geotargets-2026-10-06.csv)). Whether the Planner's own picker accepts every geo type is untested.

The second catch is API access. Developer tokens ended on 9 September 2026, and the Explorer access level now blocks the keyword planning services, so keyword data through the API needs Basic access, which needs Cloud brand verification first ([Google](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/api-policy/access-levels)). This undercuts the free Keyword Planner MCP covered later and SerpBear's Google Ads link: both look free but sit behind that gate. For one site, use the web interface.

The third catch is the AI reporting. Search Console's generative AI report reached all sites on 31 August 2026 and covers AI Overviews and AI Mode impressions by page, country, device and date ([Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports)). It has no clicks and no query dimension, it may not appear at all with too few impressions ([Search Console Help](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139?hl=en)), and a new site will likely see nothing for months. That blank is expected and does not mean anything is broken. Google also now says a site "must be included in Search generative AI features in Search Console to be eligible for display" ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)), so check that setting at launch.

Bing holds only 1.89% of Indian search against Google's 96.69% ([StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/search-engine-market-share/all/india)), but it earns its one-time setup for two reasons nobody else offers for free. Its AI Performance report shows citations and sampled grounding queries ([Bing](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview)), and its Similar Sites tab shows competitor links without verifying the competitor's site ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/bing-webmaster-tools-now-gives-you-competitive-link-data-335485)). The report does not cover ChatGPT, according to [Adobe's guide](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/brand-visibility-learn/tutorials/measurement/bing-webmaster-tools-ai-performance-report).

For technical checks, Screaming Frog's free tier crawls 500 URLs ([vendor](https://www.screamingfrog.co.uk/seo-spider/pricing/)) and has an Ubuntu arm64 build, far more than a site with five routes in three languages needs. Ahrefs Free adds 5,000 crawl credits a month on your own verified sites ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/webmaster-tools)). Field data from the CrUX API ([Chrome](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/crux/api)) will likely be empty for a new low-traffic origin, so rely on Lighthouse until PostHog has real users. Do not add a second analytics tool. Microsoft Clarity is free forever ([Microsoft](https://clarity.microsoft.com/)), but session replay collects personal data, so it would need the DPDP check that docs/PRODUCT.md requires first.

## Keyword data is thinnest exactly where your demand sits

A live autocomplete test from an Indian IP gave the most useful keyword finding in this research. Roman-script Hinglish and Devanagari both carry "cleaner's phone number" intent:`paani ki tanki saaf` respelled itself to "pani ki tanki saaf karne wale ka number", and`पानी की टंकी साफ` returned "करने वाले का नंबर". English town searches added "price list" for Chandigarh and Mohali, plus "commercial water tank cleaning mohali". Gurmukhi returned almost nothing:`ਪਾਣੀ ਦੀ ਟੈਂਕੀ` produced Pahari song lyrics and`ਪਾਣੀ ਦੀ ਟੈਂਕੀ ਸਾਫ` produced no suggestions ([test query](https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=hi&gl=in&oe=utf8&q=pani%20ki%20tanki)). These are suggestion lists from one run with no personalisation, and they are not volumes. They still tell you three things. Lead intent lives in English plus Hinglish and Devanagari. Price transparency may capture real demand, if the owner-approved price list supports it. And Gurmukhi keyword research is a dead end for now, so keep the Punjabi pages for trust and reach, and let Search Console show real demand later.

Commercial tools handle this badly. Many Hindi queries are typed in Roman script and classified as English ([Marketing SEO India](https://www.marketingseo.in/post/seo-for-hindi-websites-strategies-that-truly-work-in-india-s-regional-digital-space), a vendor blog), and DataForSEO Labs offers India only in`en` and`hi`, with no Punjabi ([OpenSEO code](https://github.com/every-app/open-seo/blob/main/src/shared/keyword-locations.ts)). The free ideation stack is therefore autocomplete, [Keyword Surfer](https://docs.surferseo.com/en/articles/6679368-keyword-surfer-extension-explained) (free, 70 countries, India included), [Keyword Tool](https://keywordtool.io/) (free suggestions across 120+ languages), [Answer Socrates](https://www.answersocrates.com/) (3 searches a day) and [AlsoAsked](https://alsoasked.com/pricing)'s free credits. Google Trends remains useful on its website, but the [Trends API](https://developers.google.com/search/apis/trends) is an alpha you have to apply for and pytrends is [reported dead](https://dev.to/esteban_ortega/pytrends-is-dead-heres-how-to-get-google-trends-data-in-2026-1a18), so skip Trends MCPs and CLIs.

If Keyword Planner gives only ranges, there are two cheap ways to get numbers. Keywords Everywhere Bronze is $7 a month billed annually at $84 (₹7,976), with credits that expire ([vendor](https://keywordseverywhere.com/pricing.html)). DataForSEO's Google Ads volume costs $0.05 to $0.09 per task of up to 1,000 keywords, and its own pages disagree on the exact rate ([DataForSEO](https://dataforseo.com/keyword-planner-api)). Its $50 minimum balance never expires ([DataForSEO](https://dataforseo.com/pricing)). Buy DataForSEO over the extension: it costs less up front, stays yours, and also funds rank tracking and SERP checks. Skip Ahrefs Starter at $29, which has a 250-row limit and no exports ([Ahrefs](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/9419051-about-ahrefs-starter-plan)), along with Semrush at [$165 a month](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/) and paid brief tools. A manual read of the top results will do for this few pages. City-level accuracy for these terms is unconfirmed in every tool, and no published volume exists for any of them, including Punjabi.

The best free keyword source arrives later. The Business Profile Performance API gives monthly search keywords that real people used to find the business ([Google](https://developers.google.com/my-business/reference/performance/rest)), but it requires a profile verified and active for 60 days ([Google](https://developers.google.com/my-business/content/prereqs)). Verify the profile early, because that clock is the one you cannot buy your way past.

## OpenSEO is a good front end to DataForSEO, and that is all it is

[OpenSEO](https://github.com/every-app/open-seo) is real software with a real shipping pace: MIT licence, 25,824 stars, v0.1.22 released on the day of this check, and a [prebuilt arm64 image](https://github.com/every-app/open-seo/blob/main/docs/SELF_HOSTING_DOCKER.md) that runs on this VPS, which has about 8.6 GiB free. Its [MCP](https://openseo.so/docs/mcp) covers keywords, SERPs, backlinks, Maps grids, AI visibility, Search Console and IndexNow. A DataForSEO key is mandatory, though, and a free mode is still an [open request](https://github.com/every-app/open-seo/issues/294). "Open-source Semrush" is marketing shorthand: data quality equals DataForSEO's.

Two details matter for you. Live rank checks silently report no position below about organic 12 ([#362](https://github.com/every-app/open-seo/issues/362)), and a new site spends months exactly there, so verify any blank by hand until that issue closes. Its [hosted plan](https://openseo.so/pricing) costs $10 a month plus 28% on every action, and it needs an active subscription despite the README's "No subscriptions", so self-host if you use it. Docker would be the first on the VPS, which makes it a server-level change under the`~/vps` handbook. The Cloudflare free route fails after about 40 audited pages ([#377](https://github.com/every-app/open-seo/issues/377)). The 25,000-star jump in six months is unexplained, since stargazer data could not be sampled, and Hacker News shows [almost no discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47926567) of it. The README's DataForSEO links are affiliate links, and so are many of its reviews ([disclosed example](https://ryandoser.com/openseo-review/)).

Your view of [rankme.fast](https://github.com/SelmiAbderrahim/rankme.fast) is fair. It has one contributor, no releases, six containers built from source and no documented arm64 support. Its own tracker reports audit findings that are false, such as assets returning HTTP 200 listed as broken ([#54](https://github.com/SelmiAbderrahim/rankme.fast/issues/54)). Its breadth suits agencies, and it uses the same paid vendors.

Better free options exist below the all-in-one suites. Every suite wraps DataForSEO-style data, while these tools work from Search Console or their own crawlers.

| Tool | Job | Cost to run | Verdict for Clean Tank |

| [crawlseo](https://github.com/crawlseo/crawlseo) (659 stars, MIT) | Search Console, PageSpeed, own crawl, autocomplete fallback, read-only MCP | VPS only | Best no-paid-data monitor; arm64 image exists; young |

SerpBear has one flaw you should know about: India is hard-coded to`hi` in [countries.ts](https://github.com/towfiqi/serpbear/blob/main/utils/countries.ts), so every Serper check sends`hl=hi` with no per-keyword choice. English-interface results are likely what most Tricity searchers see, so patch that table before trusting the numbers. How much positions differ between the two is unknown. Its README also still recommends a [dead free scraper](https://github.com/towfiqi/serpbear), and scraping Google directly from a single Oracle IP will likely get blocked, so use an API.

Rank tracking is cheap once you are past the minimum payment. Forty keywords across three cities, weekly and three pages deep, comes to about 520 checks a month. At [DataForSEO's](https://dataforseo.com/pricing/serp/google-organic-serp-api) $0.60 per 1,000 standard rate that is about $0.78 to $0.94 a month, and [Serper](https://serper.dev/) would take about $1.56, so its 2,500 free credits last about seven weeks. [SerpApi's](https://serpapi.com/pricing) 250 free monthly searches are enough for spot checks, and its location data confirms Chandigarh and Mohali targets. Google's Custom Search API is [closed to new customers](https://developers.google.com/custom-search/v1/overview), and [Brave](https://brave.com/search/api/) is not Google data. For the first three months, I would skip dedicated rank tracking altogether: Search Console already reports positions for free, and Maps rank matters more for this business.

No true open-source backlink index exists; everything real buys data. Bing's report and the [Ahrefs checker](https://ahrefs.com/backlink-checker) cover what a local site needs, and [Open PageRank](https://keywordseverywhere.com/openpagerank) gives 30,000 free domain lookups a month for a rough authority score. Avoid [All-In-One-Free-SEO-Tool](https://github.com/IamRamgarhia/All-In-One-Free-SEO-Tool), whose claim to replace ₹25,000+ a month of tools with free AI keys cannot produce real volume or link data. [Serposcope](https://github.com/serphacker/serposcope) and [Greenflare](https://github.com/beb7/gflare-tk) are dead. Repos like [trafficbot](https://github.com/lucasveneno/trafficbot) and any "private indexing network" can harm a real business site.

## Justdial and the Business Profile outrank any tool you could install

In the Tricity, Justdial is the directory that visibly matters. It has [dedicated tank-cleaning pages](https://www.justdial.com/Chandigarh/Water-Tank-Cleaning-Services-in-Mohali/nct-10532933) for Mohali and for Chandigarh sectors, and competitors there hold hundreds of reviews, such as [Shree Balaji with 392](https://www.justdial.com/Chandigarh/Shree-Balaji-Water-Tank-Cleaning-Near-Old-Gurudwara-Balongi/0172PX172-X172-210202161101-P8Z8_BZDET). The only direct test of Indian AI answers found the same pattern. [NicoDigital](https://www.nicodigital.com/seo/ai-engines-local-business-search-test/) ran 15 prompts on four engines through DataForSEO's API, one run each, and Justdial appeared in 25 of 48 Indian answers against 0 of 12 international ones: Perplexity 10 of 12, Gemini 9, ChatGPT 6, Claude 0. Third-party platforms took 37% of Indian citation slots against 6% abroad. This is a small agency test with no Punjab cities, used API output, and is the only such evidence available, but it agrees with what the Justdial pages show. Start with the free listing, judge any paid package on leads alone, and expect sales pressure. No 2026 package prices or independent lead-quality data were found. [IndiaMART](https://dir.indiamart.com/chandigarh/water-tank-cleaning-services.html) comes second, for housing societies and hospitals, on reasoning alone, since no citation data exists for it. [Urban Company](https://urbancompany.com/chandigarh-plumbers) is a competitor, not a citation source. A single search found no Sulekha, NoBroker or TradeIndia tank-cleaning pages for the area, which needs a manual check before you write them off.

Global data makes the same point from another direction. [BrightLocal's](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-ai-visibility-study/) study of about 200,000 local prompts, mostly US, found business websites took 42% of citations and Business Profiles 28.6%. ChatGPT relied little on the Business Profile and leaned on Yelp and Bing, so Bing Places and directories matter separately for ChatGPT, which holds 77.32% of India's measured AI referrals ([StatCounter](https://gs.statcounter.com/ai-chatbot-market-share/all/india)). That figure counts referrals, not users. Google's own guidance names Business Profiles as helping visibility in AI responses ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)).

Rank grids work anywhere with cheap entry points. [Geogrid.dev](https://geogrid.dev/tools/local-rank-check) gives 3 free no-account checks a day on a 3×3 grid, and a free account gets 200 credits once, then 50 a month, though India is not named. [Local Falcon](https://www.localfalcon.com/pricing) says it works [everywhere in any language](https://www.localfalcon.com/knowledge-base/kb4-what-localities-does-this-rank-scanner-work-in), and its pay-as-you-go credits at $0.05 never expire, so a 5×5 scan costs about ₹119. One 5×5 monthly across Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula and Zirakpur uses 100 credits, so rotate towns on the free tier or top up. No grid tool has an independent accuracy test in India. [Outscraper](https://outscraper.com/pricing/) gives 500 free places and reviews every 30 days, which is plenty to study competitors' reviews, and [Whitespark's free Citation Finder](https://www.whitespark.ca/local-citation-finder/) allows 3 searches a day, with Indian coverage unconfirmed. Avoid incentivised review drives: Google added a guideline on fake and undisclosed incentivized reviews in July 2026 ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/updates)).

## AI answers reward ordinary SEO plus off-site presence, so measure cheaply

The evidence on what earns citations is less exciting than the tool market suggests. Google says you do not need AI text files or special markup, and that AEO and GEO are "still SEO" ([Google](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)), though Google has a stake in that message and speaks only for its own search. An [SE Ranking](https://seranking.com/blog/llms-txt/) study of nearly 300,000 domains found no measurable link between llms.txt and citations, and [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/llmstxt-study/) found 97% of the files got zero requests in a month. In the best-controlled schema test, [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/schema-ai-citations/) matched 1,885 pages that added JSON-LD against controls and found no lift: AI Overviews moved −4.6%, AI Mode +2.4% and ChatGPT +2.2%, the last two indistinguishable from zero. Those pages were already heavily cited, so the test says nothing about an unknown local business, where schema may still help engines resolve who you are. Brand mentions correlated 0.664 with AI Overview visibility against 0.218 for backlinks ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)), which is correlation from big brands and shows direction only. No causal study exists for local service businesses in any country, and none was found linking citations to leads. The Princeton paper's headline of [up to 40%](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735) comes from its authors' own benchmark and has no independent replication on live engines.

Measurement has a real limit. Two runs of the same prompt have under a 1-in-100 chance of giving the same brand list, though the set of brands considered is fairly stable ([SparkToro via SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-recommendations-change-with-nearly-every-query-sparktoro/566242)). A business reappears about half the time on repeat runs ([BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-ai-visibility-study/)). API answers also differ from what people see: [Surfer's](https://surfer.ai/blog/llm-scraped-ai-answers-vs-api-results/) study of 13,779 answers found brand overlap of about 21 to 32% even after name matching, and Surfer sells UI-based tracking. So the honest metric is the share of runs that mention or cite you, per engine and language, never a position. The list of cited domains is worth more than any score, because it tells you which directories to get onto.

For a DIY API tracker of 30 prompts in 3 languages, four engines cost about $3 for one monthly run, $14 for five runs and $37 for three weekly runs, based on current list prices and an assumed 5,000 input tokens. Multi-search answers could double the OpenAI and Claude lines, and Gemini fits inside its free [500 grounded requests a day](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing). Money is not the problem, validity is. API calls have no confirmed Chandigarh location, so "near me" prompts may answer for the wrong place. Consumer-UI scraping is reported to conflict with [OpenAI's terms](https://www.itechguides.com/?p=866691), which could not be read directly, and tools that automate your logged-in sessions, such as [OneGlanse](https://github.com/oneglanse/oneglanse), are brittle as well.

Start the hand panel smaller than the research notes suggest. Their 10 prompts, 3 languages, 3 surfaces and 3 repeats come to 270 answers a month, which nobody will keep doing. Five prompts in three languages on ChatGPT and Google AI Mode, twice each, is 60 answers. Run it once now, since competitors and directories are already being named, and use the result as your baseline. Put the location in the prompt ("in Mohali") and write it the way people type, Hinglish included. Match your brand in Latin, Devanagari and Gurmukhi.

Punjabi is the weak link across all of this. Google's AI Mode announcements added Hindi and seven other Indian languages, with [no Punjabi named](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/supercharging-search-for-india-new-languages-in-ai-mode-search-live-debuts/), though those sources date from 2025. The only tracker reported to name Punjabi is AnswerTrace on its top plan, according to a [competitor's page](https://depra.ai/best-ai-visibility-tools-india). No data exists on what gets cited in Hindi or Punjabi answers. A hand test will tell you more than any vendor can.

For crawlers, a robots.txt pass proves little on this host. [Vercel's AI Bots ruleset](https://examples.vercel.com/kb/guide/how-to-utilize-vercels-bot-management-features) can block a crawler that robots.txt permits, so request the site with each bot's user agent and look for a 200. [Vercel Drains](https://vercel.com/docs/drains) need the Pro plan, which is why a small user-agent log in`src/proxy.ts` is the better route. It should hold no personal data, but check that against docs/PRODUCT.md. [ChatGPT-User](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) hits are the closest available sign that a real person's question reached a page.

## Install one skill pack and a few MCP servers, and read them first

No SEO plugin exists in Anthropic's [official marketplace](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-plugins-official/blob/main/.claude-plugin/marketplace.json). SEO lives in the [community marketplace](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugins/anthropic-marketplaces.md) and authors' own repos, which Anthropic does not vouch for. The one public benchmark tempers expectations: on a fixture site with planted defects, the no-skill baseline scored 73.0, against 84.9 for the best entrant, with about ±8 points of run-to-run noise ([seo-skill-bench](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aleclindz/seo-skill-bench/main/README.md)). Its maintainer is an entrant, and claude-seo and marketingskills were not in the table read. Skills give Claude a checklist. Your Search Console data and INFO-BANK facts are what make the output useful, and all of these packs are tuned for English sites, so Hindi and Punjabi work needs human review.

Stay away from anything that puts a third party between you and your Google accounts or breaks someone's bot checks. [Ryze's open-seo-mcp-skills](https://github.com/Ryze-AI-Adgent/open-seo-mcp-skills) has 8,220 stars and only 14 files, and its plugin wires your Google data into a hosted connector with no published pricing. [irinabuht12-oss/marketing-skills](https://github.com/irinabuht12-oss/marketing-skills) follows the same funnel and has no licence. [notfair-plugin](https://github.com/nowork-studio/notfair-plugin) registers a hosted server with write access. [cnych/seo-mcp](https://github.com/cnych/seo-mcp) and [dataseo-mcp](https://github.com/egebese/dataseo-mcp) pay CAPTCHA solvers to reach Ahrefs data. [akii](https://github.com/akii-technologies-ltd/akii-seo-ai-search-optimizer) posts telemetry from a session hook. [geo-seo-claude](https://github.com/zubair-trabzada/geo-seo-claude) is usable only if you skip its curl-to-bash install. Community Business Profile servers such as [mcp-google-business](https://github.com/A1-x-Tech/mcp-google-business) hold write access to your public listing and reviews with almost no users, so manage the profile by hand. [ahrefs-mcp-server](https://github.com/ahrefs/ahrefs-mcp-server) and [mcpdoc](https://github.com/langchain-ai/mcpdoc) are archived. Google has official servers for [GA4](https://github.com/googleanalytics/google-analytics-mcp) and [Ads](https://github.com/googleads/google-ads-mcp), but none was found for Search Console, Business Profile or Bing.

The risk is real because a plugin can "execute arbitrary code on your machine with your user privileges", and hooks and MCP servers run outside the sandbox ([Anthropic](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugins/security.md)). [Snyk](https://snyk.io/blog/toxicskills-malicious-ai-agent-skills-clawhub) reported 76 confirmed malicious skills among 3,984 scanned, as cited secondhand since the page was not fetched, and [postmark-mcp](https://www.thaicert.or.th/?p=9695) showed a clean package turning malicious in one update. A stdio server can read`.env` files whatever Claude's permissions say, and this VPS hosts other services too. So read every SKILL.md, hook and script you use, copy single skills at a reviewed commit, pin versions, never use`@latest` for anything holding credentials, keep Google scopes read-only in a separate Cloud project, and prefer local servers to hosted ones. Static greps of 14 repos found nothing malicious, which is a shallow result and not an audit. Most packs give Claude no written defence against instructions planted in a page it audits, so be careful pointing them at competitors' sites.

A small repo-specific skill in`.agents/skills`, written from the packs above, will likely beat any of them, because it can know Payload, the`[lang]` routes and INFO-BANK.

## The repo needs little code, and AEO-GEO.md needs five corrections

Stay on native Next.js metadata and sitemaps. [next-sitemap](https://www.npmjs.com/package/next-sitemap) has not changed since 2023 and adds nothing to the existing`sitemap.ts`, and [next-seo](https://www.npmjs.com/package/next-seo) is only a JSON-LD helper now. The one worthwhile addition is [schema-dts](https://www.npmjs.com/package/schema-dts) as a dev dependency, for the JSON-LD helper the docs already call for.`src/` has no`ld+json` output yet. [@payloadcms/plugin-seo](https://payloadcms.com/docs/plugins/seo) would add little, since pages already have`metaTitle` and`metaDescription`. IndexNow is one POST gated on`isLive` and published status, sent from an`afterChange` hook, with low value and low cost: [Google is not a listed endpoint](https://www.indexnow.org/faq). JSON-LD escaping follows the guide in`node_modules/next/dist/docs/`.

Validate schema by hand with the [Schema Markup Validator](https://validator.schema.org/) and Rich Results Test, or from a Playwright check, because no trustworthy validator MCP turned up. [schema-org-mcp](https://github.com/Theycallmeholla/schema-org-mcp) exists but is untested, and whether the public tools may be scripted was not checked.

This research contradicts`docs/AEO-GEO.md` in five places. Gap 1 calls JSON-LD the largest gap, but for AI citations the evidence ranks off-site confirmation above it, so keep the schema because it is cheap and move gaps 2 and 4 ahead. Gap 2 says Gemini and ChatGPT both pull from map data, which BrightLocal contradicts for ChatGPT. Gap 3 says FAQ rich results survive for government and health sites, but they [ended on 7 May 2026](https://developers.google.com/search/updates), and its claim that the markup labels Q&A "for every other engine" has no evidence behind it, so the visible answers matter more. Gap 5 can change from low priority to skip. Gap 10 should name Search Console's generative AI report and Bing AI Performance, and the checklist should add the generative AI features setting and move Justdial to first place after launch. Keep the doc's ban on review markup: one set of notes suggests adding genuine Review markup, and the owner's rule wins.

## Conclusion

The market sells visibility dashboards, but a pre-launch local business has nothing yet to display on them. The scarce inputs for Clean Tank are verified listings, real reviews, plain facts in three languages and time, and none of those are for sale at any price on this list. That turns the budget question around. The right order is to do the unglamorous free work, let Search Console and Business Profile keywords show what Tricity customers actually type, and only then spend ₹4,750 on data aimed at a question you already have.

The open questions are ones only you can answer cheaply, and answering them would improve on anything published. Run Keyword Planner on your account with Punjabi 1110 and the Tricity geo IDs, and put the 60-answer panel on an Indian phone this month. Those two checks will produce the first real evidence that exists anywhere on Hindi and Punjabi demand and citations for water tank cleaning in the Tricity.

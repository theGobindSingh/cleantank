# Product

What this site is, who it serves and what it may say. Agents read this before deciding pages, copy or structure.

Facts, figures, legal text, contact details, client names and website-safe long copy come from `docs/ref/INFO-BANK.md`, which the owner approved. When this file and INFO-BANK disagree on a fact, INFO-BANK wins.

## The business

Clean Tank Services does scientific and mechanized cleaning and disinfection of water storage tanks for homes, housing societies, institutions, commercial sites and industry. It is an initiative of Gobind Galaxy, the parent firm and legal entity, based in Kanpur. Founded 2008; founder and CEO is Mr. Gurdev Singh.

Naming:

- Brand: Clean Tank Services ("Clean Tank" in running text). Formal lockup: "CLEAN TANK SERVICES — An Initiative of GOBIND GALAXY".
- Descriptor: "Scientific & Mechanized Water Tank Cleaning". It is not the tagline and the two are never swapped. "Mechanized" always takes a z.
- Tagline: "Clean Tank, Healthy Life".
- Franchise operators appear as "Authorised Service Provider" under the master brand, with their name and city in a text slot. They never become the primary brand and get no sub-brand.
- Clients named "Kanha Galaxy" and "Kanak Galaxy" have nothing to do with Gobind Galaxy.

### Services

- Tank cleaning by type: domestic (up to 20,000 L), commercial overhead (up to 20,00,000 L), commercial underground (up to 1 crore L), institutional, large-capacity.
- AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract): scheduled cleanings, advance reminders and a service history. Terms are agreed per customer.
- 24×7 emergency service, always with "subject to location, manpower and operational availability".
- Franchise and business opportunity (see Claims below for the only figures allowed).

There is no public price list. Say prices depend on scope, capacity, location and site.

How often to clean depends on the organisation (hospitals about every 3 months, schools about every 6, general guidance 3 to 6 months). Never publish a single flat interval.

### The 5-step process

The process is the product. Always show five steps, in this order, with these names (they match the images in `public/assets/images/process/`):

1. De-watering: water removed with mechanical pumps.
2. High-pressure cleaning: walls, floor and roof cleaned with a high-pressure water jet (up to 250 bar, subject to site and equipment).
3. Sludge & water removal: with an industrial vacuum.
4. Disinfection: antibacterial agent.
5. UV treatment: marked "where applicable".

The old 6-step version is retired. After the service the customer gets a Cleaning Certificate and a Cleaning Date Sticker on the tank with the cleaning date and the next due date. Follow-up comes about a week before that date. The system recommends and the customer decides.

## Users

- Homeowners and families. Often a joint decision, triggered by dirt, smell, insects or a long gap since the last cleaning. They want the process explained simply and they worry whether the chemicals are safe for drinking water. They find the service through Google, referrals and social media, and many think in Hindi.
- Housing societies, RWAs and apartments. Many tanks in one place.
- Institutions: hospitals, schools, colleges and universities, government. The buyer is usually engineering or maintenance staff, with admin and procurement. They need the method, trained teams, documentation, multi-tank scheduling and reliability, usually under an AMC. Government work comes through tenders and GeM.
- Franchise partners. Local entrepreneurs with a service mindset and limited capital who want a proven system, training and support.

The real competitor is the local plumber or labourer, and doing nothing. Never criticise either. The question every page should answer is "What difference will your service actually make?"

Conversion goals, in order: service enquiry, AMC enquiry, franchise enquiry. Channels are phone, WhatsApp, the website and the enquiry form. An enquiry asks only for what planning needs: name, location, tank type, capacity, requirement and time since the last cleaning.

## Voice

The persona is "The Humble Expert": a Sage/Expert (about 70%) with some Caregiver (about 30%). Traits: knowledgeable, disciplined, humble, responsible, reassuring. Write like a specialist who wants the customer to understand the work, explaining technical detail simply instead of hiding it or making it sound complicated.

- Calm and plain. No exclamation marks, no urgency, no fear.
- Formal and precise with institutions, warm with families, structured with franchise partners.
- Words that fit: clean, scientific, process, professional, hygiene, treatment, method, system, properly, documented, specialist, care, trust.
- Use carefully and only with evidence: health, safe, non-toxic, protection.
- Don't use: cheap, best, #1, guaranteed, 100% bacteria-free, revolutionary, international, global.

Examples:

| Avoid | Write |
|---|---|
| YOUR WATER COULD BE DANGEROUS! | When was your water tank last cleaned? |
| We are the BEST tank cleaners! | Professional tank cleaning built around a disciplined process. |
| Book NOW before it's too late! | If your tank is due for cleaning, we can help you plan the service. |
| Small investment, high profit! | A structured service business you can build in your district, with training and support. |

Before publishing a line, ask: would the Humble Expert say this face to face, can we prove it, and does it respect the customer's right to decide?

Reusable lines: master message "Clean Tank Services provides scientific and mechanized water-storage-tank cleaning through knowledgeable people, disciplined processes and professional execution." Purpose "To make hygiene a part of everyday life." Vision "Clean & Healthy Nation". Website-safe long copy for About, Gobind Galaxy, Water Hygiene and Look Inside Your Tank is in INFO-BANK §6; prefer it over the flyer copy.

## Claims

Never invent a certification, approval, lab result, health claim, client, statistic or legal status. If a page needs a fact that isn't in INFO-BANK, leave a marked gap and ask.

Safe to state:

- Established 2008, "15+ years".
- "ISO 9001:2015 certified (Q2A-UK Certification Board), Certificate No. Q2A-2026-0401T118011, valid to 31 March 2029." Never "accredited", "internationally recognised", UKAS or IAF.
- Operating states: Punjab, Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Jharkhand, Maharashtra.
- GSTIN 09LFS1473F5ZU.

Needs a document before use: non-toxic, biodegradable, eco-friendly, how long the treatment lasts, the CPWD DSR listing (exact edition and item), the "60% of diseases are water-borne" figure, the Delhi Railway Station story.

Every "safe" or "bacteria-free" line carries the qualifier that water quality also depends on source water, plumbing, tank condition and upkeep after the service.

Franchise pages may use only these figures, with the financial disclaimer from INFO-BANK §8 directly beside them: investment ₹3 to 8 lakh, royalty 3.5% per month as applicable to the model, indicative ROI 40% or above subject to actual performance, payback 4 to 6 months, office about 150 sq ft or home-based, 3-year agreement. Don't state what ROI or royalty is calculated on, what each model includes, or territory exclusivity. The retired franchise copy listed in INFO-BANK (guaranteed income, event offers, "06-stage", "Remember business is for PROFIT!") never appears.

Client names and logos need the client's permission before they go live. The list is in INFO-BANK; keep its spellings. IIT Delhi is a client (INFO-BANK lists it under "IITs across India"), and its logo is in `public/assets/images/clients/institutional/`.

Contact details come only from INFO-BANK §12. There is no WhatsApp number yet; don't make one up.

## Avoid

- Looking like a generic cleaning company, a plumber's flyer or a trade-show standee.
- Fear and shock: dead animals, filthy-tank close-ups as scare tactics, disease talk. The before/after images in `public/assets/images/compare/` are evidence, so show them calmly.
- Sales pressure: countdowns, limited slots, discount badges, festive offers.
- Corporate distance or claims to be international.
- Attacks on competitors.

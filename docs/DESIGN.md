# Design

The visual system for Clean Tank Services. Read it before any visual work. The code in `src/styles` follows it; when you change one, change the other. `/design` on the running site shows all of it rendered from the live CSS: every ramp and role with measured hex and contrast, the type scale at the current width, fonts, both modes side by side, and every component.

## Brand & style

"Quiet precision. One standard, every tank." Pages should read like a lab report or a spec sheet with room to breathe: ruled, numbered, exact and calm. Authority comes from the visuals and humility from the words.

The test: cover the logo and the page should still read as a ruled spec sheet with a navy band, one coral marker and a lot of white space.

References: a hospital lab bench, a calibrated pressure gauge, test reports, dated inspection tags, Swiss technical signage (navy plus one marker color), technicians in pressed navy uniforms.

Never: gradients (one exception: the hero scrim, below), shadows, gloss, glass or blur, glow; water splashes, droplets, waves, bubbles; sun rays, bokeh, lens flare; cartoon bacteria, mascots, 3D tanks; red or orange urgency badges, starbursts, seals, shields; countdowns; money imagery on franchise pages; stock handshakes and posed smiles; pills or any radius above 6 px (user's call, 4 Nov 2026); tricolour layouts or festival motifs; fake ISO or approval marks.

## Tokens

`src/styles/colors.css` holds the color ramps as HSL triplets (`--color-primary-700-base: 213 40% 21%`), plus the dark-mode and band overrides. `src/styles/globals.css` turns each triplet into a color in `@theme static` (`--color-primary-700: hsl(var(--color-primary-700-base))`) and defines the semantic roles on top, so every one is also a Tailwind utility. Tailwind's own palette, radii, shadows and breakpoints are removed; only the brand values exist.

- Ramps. Every family has stops 50 to 950 and they are the same in every mode:
  - `primary`: steel-navy, 700 = `#20334B` (locked).
  - `accent`: coral, 500 = `#EE7555` (locked).
  - `secondary`: indigo slate, 700 = `#484474`. 700 is text, rules and fills, 100 soft fill, 500 border, 300 text in dark mode.
  - `grey`: warm stone (50 `#F5F4F1`) to graphite (900 `#25282A`). 500 is the lightest control border and 600 the lightest text; 400 and lighter are decorative only.
  - `success`, `caution`, `info`, `error`: 700 is text, 100 soft fill, 500 border and icon, 300 text in dark mode.
  - `white` on its own. There is no black.
- Semantic roles. Components use these; dark mode and bands re-map them:

| Utility | Light | Job |
|---|---|---|
| `bg` / `bg-subtle` | white / grey 50 | Page ground, alternate sections |
| `surface` | white | Cards, panels, inputs |
| `band` | primary 700 | Header, footer, emphasis bands, table headers |
| `fg` / `fg-muted` | grey 900 / grey 600 | Body text / muted text |
| `heading` | primary 700 | Headings |
| `link`, `link-hover` | primary 700 / 800 | Links, always underlined |
| `border` | grey 300 | Decorative 1 px rules, `Hr` |
| `border-control` | grey 500 | Input and control borders (3:1) |
| `border-strong` | primary 700 | Structural rules, table frames |
| `primary`, `primary-hover`, `primary-active`, `on-primary` | primary 700 / 600 / 800, white | Button fill and label |
| `marker` / `marker-text` | accent 500 / 700 | Level Line and bars / small marker text and the one key figure |
| `secondary`, `secondary-bg`, `secondary-border` | secondary 700 / 100 / 500 | Category tags, heading rules, chart series, soft panels |
| `focus` | primary 700 | Focus ring |
| `disabled`, `disabled-bg` | grey 500 / 100 | Disabled controls |
| `success`, `caution`, `info`, `error` (+ `-bg`, `-border`) | 700 / 100 / 500 | Status components only |

So `bg-bg`, `text-fg-muted`, `border-border-control`, `bg-primary`, `text-heading`.

- Dark mode. The meta tag described in docs/CONVENTIONS.md switches the roles to a navy set: `bg` primary 900, `bg-subtle` 800, `surface` 700, `fg` primary 50, headings white, primary buttons white with a navy label, state and secondary text at 300. It is never black and never an inverted ramp. Light is the default, even when the OS prefers dark; dark mode applies only after the visitor picks it.
- Band scope. Put `data-surface="band"` on any navy band. It fills the band and re-maps the roles inside it (white text and links, white focus ring, white-fill buttons with navy labels), in either mode. Components inside need no changes. For the secondary emphasis band, also set the fill: `data-surface="band"` with `className="[--color-band:var(--color-secondary-700)]"`.
- Preview scopes. `data-scheme="light"` or `data-scheme="dark"` on any element shows that mode inside it, whatever the page theme. `/design` uses them to put both modes side by side. The light scope repeats the light roles, and `src/styles/colors.test.ts` fails if the copies differ.
- Alpha. Tailwind modifiers (`bg-primary-900/70`) or `hsl(var(--color-primary-900-base) / 0.7)` in CSS. The only translucent colors are the photo scrim (primary 900 at 80%, fading out) and the modal scrim (primary 900 at 60%). No translucent accent.
- Radius. `rounded-none` 0 (tables, bands, ruled cells, full-bleed images), `rounded-xs` 2 px (step nodes, checkbox, radio, grid containers), `rounded-sm` 6 px (buttons, inputs, cards, alerts, menus, image frames, the audience doors and the price list; user's call, 4 Nov 2026). Nothing else exists, including `rounded-full`.
- Shadows. None. `shadow-*` generates nothing. Depth comes from 1 px rules, ruled cells, solid bands and white-to-stone steps.
- Breakpoints. `sm` 360, `md` 768, `lg` 1024, `xl` 1280, `2xl` 1440. Mobile first.
- Fonts (`src/styles/fonts.ts`, loaded with `next/font`):
  - `font-sans`: Anek Latin, for body, buttons and UI.
  - `font-heading`: Anek Latin at `font-stretch: 78%`, for display to h3.
  - `font-mono`: IBM Plex Mono 500 and 600, for labels, reference codes and record values.
  - `font-serif`: IBM Plex Serif. Reserved, not in the launch pages; for long-form reading such as a certificate or legal text.
  - `font-cursive`: Caveat. Reserved, not in the launch pages; for a signature or a handwritten note, never headings or body.
  - Any face may change later, so components use only these tokens, never a family name.
  - Anek Devanagari and Anek Gurmukhi follow Anek Latin in the sans, heading and mono stacks, so Hindi and Punjabi text picks its own face. A page downloads an Indic font only when it has text in that script.
- Type scale. `--fs-*` in `globals.css`, fluid between 360 and 1280 px (below). The text components in `src/components/html` take these names as `$size`.
- Spacing. Tailwind's 4 px unit, restricted to the brand steps: 0, 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96, 128 px (`1 2 3 4 6 8 10 12 16 20 24 32`). Other steps (5, 7, 9, 14) are off-scale.

### Color rules

- Proportion: neutral (white and stone) about 60%, navy about 30%, accent at most 10% of non-photo area. On the website, navy is only the header, footer, at most two emphasis bands, the audience doors and buttons. Never a whole navy page.
- Accent is a marker only: the Level Line, the active step (and the price list's reached discount step, outlined in accent 600; user's call, 4 Nov 2026), reference tags and index numbers, one key figure per view, the hero's highlighted words, the highlighted words of any other block heading (accent 500, which fails contrast on light grounds; owner's calls, 30 and 31 Oct 2026). Never a button fill (two exceptions: the value proposition button and the price list's filled button, below), link, body text, alert, error, warning or urgency signal. `#EE7555` on white is 2.87:1 and fails; small accent text uses `marker-text`.
- Secondary is navy's quiet partner: category tags (`bg-secondary-bg text-secondary border-secondary-border`), the rule beside a heading, the second chart series after navy, soft panels, and at most one emphasis band per page. Its area comes out of navy's 30%. Never a button, link, focus ring, status or alert, and never beside a state color in a way that could read as one.
- Every state color comes with an icon and a word.
- Text on photos only in the hero, white, over an accent 950 scrim that stays solid behind the text: 50% from `lg` up, 85% below it, where the text sits over busier parts of the photos.

## Typography

Anek for everything, IBM Plex Mono for labels and codes. No italics anywhere (Anek has none; emphasis is weight 600) and no synthetic bold. Two widths only: 78% for display to h3, 100% for everything else. Always set `font-weight` and `font-stretch` explicitly, because Anek's default instance is 500.

Fluid between 360 and 1280 px, ratio 1.2. The `--fs-*` token is the `$size` name in `src/components/html`:

| Token | Size px (360 to 1280) | `clamp()` | Style |
|---|---|---|---|
| `display` (hero only) | 54 up to 800 px wide, then 6.75vw (69 at 1024, 130 at 1920), capped at 135 from 2000 px | `calc(var(--fs-statement) * 1.125)` | heading face, 78%, 700, uppercase, +0.01em, line height 1.00. Always 1.125 times `statement`, so the hero is slightly bigger than a section header at every width (owner's call, 31 Oct 2026) |
| `statement` (the standard section header and inner-page H1) | 48 up to 800 px wide, then 6vw (61 at 1024, 115 at 1920), capped at 120 from 2000 px | `clamp(3rem, 6vw, 7.5rem)` | same as h2 but line height 0.85 (a `leading-[0.85]` class, so the Hindi and Punjabi heading leading still wins). The cap is where the content stops widening (1800 px at 2000 px wide). The minimum keeps the headline's longer line inside a 320 px phone in all three languages (about 5.2em in Punjabi) |
| `h1` (status pages) | 33.18 to 37.32 | `clamp(2.074rem, 1.972rem + 0.451vw, 2.333rem)` | same as display |
| `h2` (the closing band) | 27.65 to 31.10 | `clamp(1.728rem, 1.643rem + 0.376vw, 1.944rem)` | same, line height 1.02 |
| `h3` | 23.04 to 25.92 | `clamp(1.44rem, 1.37rem + 0.313vw, 1.62rem)` | same, line height 1.05 |
| `h4` | 19.20 to 21.60 | `clamp(1.2rem, 1.141rem + 0.261vw, 1.35rem)` | 100%, 600, sentence case, line height 1.30 |
| `body-l` | 19.20 to 21.60 | same as h4 | 400, line height 1.50 |
| `body` | 16 to 18 | `clamp(1rem, 0.951rem + 0.217vw, 1.125rem)` | 400, line height 1.50 |
| `small` | 14 to 15 | `clamp(0.875rem, 0.851rem + 0.109vw, 0.938rem)` | 400, +0.01em |
| `caption` | 12 to 13 | `clamp(0.75rem, 0.726rem + 0.109vw, 0.813rem)` | 400, +0.01em; also label and value (Plex Mono 500 / 600, uppercase, +0.08em) |
| `ref` | 11 to 12 | `clamp(0.6875rem, 0.663rem + 0.109vw, 0.75rem)` | Plex Mono 500, uppercase, +0.10em |
| button | 14 (fixed) | `0.875rem` | Anek 700, uppercase, +0.06em |

`h1` to `h3` get the heading face, 78% width, caps, tracking and `heading` color from `src/styles/defaults.css`. Headings use caps down to h3 only, at most 8 words; write the source text in sentence case and uppercase it with CSS. Never uppercase paragraphs, lists, captions, URLs, email addresses, the descriptor line or Indic text. Line length 60 to 68ch (45 to 75 for body). Left-aligned, ragged right, never justified; `text-wrap: balance` on headings and `pretty` on body. Nothing smaller than 12 px except `ref`. Curly quotes, no em dashes in brand copy, ranges written "to" ("₹3 to 8 lakh").

Hindi and Punjabi:

- Anek Devanagari and Anek Gurmukhi, at 100% width, with no caps and no letter-spacing ever. `defaults.css` enforces this on anything inside `lang="hi"` or `lang="pa"`, and it beats utilities like `uppercase`.
- Indic headings are 700 at the same px size as the Latin one. Line height: body 1.65 (hi) and 1.60 (pa), headings 1.35 (hi) and 1.30 (pa), highlighted words inside them included. Set a Latin heading's own line height with a class, not `$lineHeight`: the inline style would beat the Indic one. Indic measure 35 to 60 characters.
- Plex Mono has no Indic glyphs, so an Indic label falls back to Anek at the same size.
- Mixed lines ("Tank साफ करवाना है"): keep each word in its own script and wrap the Indic part in `lang="hi"` or `lang="pa"`. On a Hindi or Punjabi page, wrap Latin words that should keep their caps (the brand name, reference codes) in `lang="en"`.
- Digits are Western Arabic (0 to 9) in all three languages.

Numbers: Indian grouping (`20,00,000`), a non-breaking space before units (`20,000 L`, `250 bar`), `₹` with no space, tabular figures in tables and records. Dates as "03 Oct 2026". Phones as +91 and two groups of five digits (`formatPhone` in `@utils/phone`).

## Layout

- Max content width 1800 px, set by `CommonFullWidthWrapper` (docs/CONVENTIONS.md, Styling): side margins are 5% of the width each, until the content reaches 1800 px. Columns 4 (base), 8 (`md`), 12 (`lg` up). Gutters 16 then 24.
- Sections alternate white and stone with a 1 px rule between them. Section gaps 32 / 40 / 48 px by breakpoint (owner's call, 31 Oct 2026).
- Standard section header: `@components/section-headline`, an H2 at the `statement` size set in two lines (owner's call, 31 Oct 2026: every section header uses this one size). `@components/section` always renders it, with an optional `highlight` for words in accent 500; blocks that lay out their own columns use `SectionHeadline` directly. It splits the text into two lines at any width, and a long heading wraps further on small phones without overflowing; each section's body sits under its heading. Strips are the exception (owner's call, 31 Oct 2026): the clients band, the trust band and the closing band keep their own smaller headings and don't use `@components/section`. The page H1 of an inner page is set at `statement` too, so it never ranks below an H2. A hero's H1 stays at `display`, which is 1.125 times `statement` at every width (owner's call, 31 Oct 2026). H3s and card titles keep their sizes.
- Heading punctuation: hero and section headings, strips included, end in punctuation: a period (danda in Hindi and Punjabi) by default, or a question mark where the heading is a question (owner's call, 31 Oct 2026). Page titles don't need it.
- Heading highlight: every block with a heading has the optional, localized `highlight` field (`highlightField` in `src/blocks/highlight.ts`), a comma-separated list of words or phrases from the heading, which `@components/highlight` shows in accent 500 in every mode (owner's call, 31 Oct 2026). Spaces after the commas are optional, an empty field shows no accent, and each language needs its own list, matching its own heading. The founder's H2 is the person's name, so that block has no highlight; H3s, card titles and step names have none either.
- Each section gets a number and a mono reference tag above its H2 (`02  REF CT-WEB-02`, `ref` style, `marker-text`). The number counts the sections after the hero, from 01, in page order. The 5-step track has a visible H2 but no number or tag (owner's call).
- Above the fold, at most five elements: headline, one line, CTAs, the before/after comparison, brand mark.
- Header (`src/components/header`): navy band, logo top-left (never centred), then Home, Solutions, Process, About, Contact and Franchise in button type (user's call, 4 Nov 2026), current page marked with a 3 px accent underline and `aria-current="page"` (Home only on the home page), then the language dropdown, all in one `<nav>`. Solutions (`solutions.tsx`) is a disclosure (WAI-ARIA APG Disclosure Navigation Menu, never `role="menu"`): a button with a chevron opens a panel under it with Homes and Organisations, each a label with one `fg-muted` line under it from the dictionaries. The panel is its own navy band, `rounded-sm`, 1 px `border`, no shadow, and fades in with 4 px of travel in 150 ms. Escape (focus back to the button), a click outside, tabbing out or following a link close it; it doesn't open on hover. Its links stay in the HTML while it is closed, hidden with `visibility`. On `/homes` or `/organisations` the button gets the accent underline and the matching link `aria-current="page"` and the underline on its label. The theme toggle (`src/components/theme-toggle`) is hidden from the header for now. Fixed to the top of the viewport, 64 px tall; the body is padded by the same amount. Below `lg` (1024 px, phones and tablets) the same link panel becomes a sidenav (styled in `header.css`) that slides in from the right over the modal scrim. In it Home comes first and the Solutions button is hidden: "Solutions" is a plain `fg-muted` label with Homes and Organisations under it, indented 16 px past a 1 px `border` rule on the left, without their lines. One burger button opens and closes it, drawing its three lines into an X while open; Escape, a click on the scrim or following a link also close it. While it's open, Tab cycles between the burger and the panel's controls. The burger is a 36 px icon button (`iconButtonStyles`) with a 44 px hit area.
- Mobile (under 768): the contact bar (`src/components/contact-bar`), fixed to the bottom on every page, with Call (filled) and WhatsApp (outline) buttons side by side on `surface` under a 1 px `border` rule, respecting `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`. `--contact-bar-height` in `defaults.css` (0 from `md` up) pads the body and comes off the hero's phone height, so the bar never covers the hero's buttons or the footer's end.
- Footer (`src/components/footer`): navy band of ruled cells. The first row holds the brand (lockup, Gobind Galaxy endorsement, ISO certificate and GSTIN as a definition list), the site map, the contacts (call, WhatsApp, email) and the legal links (Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, and Cookie settings while analytics runs). The second row is the correspondence office; the registered office is left out here and on `/contact` (owner's call; `shownOffices` in `src/site.ts`). Under the grid: the copyright line. Cell headings are mono caps in `fg-muted`. Footer links drop the underline until hover; on phones the site map runs in two columns.
- Consent banner (`src/components/consent-banner`): a modal `<dialog>` pinned to the bottom edge, over the modal scrim. The page behind is inert and Lenis is stopped, so it doesn't scroll; Escape doesn't close it. The visitor has to accept or decline to go on.

- Reference tag: plain mono caps text, no box. One accent tag per view at most; the rest use `fg-muted`.
- Step track (the 5-step process): five square 40 px nodes (32 px at 360), `rounded-xs`, 2 px outline, Plex Mono numerals 01 to 05, 2 px connectors with square elbows. ## Motion

Functional only: exact, brief, still. Durations 70 / 110 / 150 / 240 ms (240 is the ceiling). Easing `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0.38, 0.9)` standard, `cubic-bezier(0, 0, 0.38, 0.9)` entrance, `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 1, 0.9)` exit. Animate only opacity and transform, with at most 8 px of travel. One exception (owner's call): the header sidenav slides its full width in 240 ms with the entrance easing while the scrim fades in, and the burger's lines morph into an X in 500 ms with `cubic-bezier(0.645, 0.045, 0.355, 1)`, copied from the earlier site; and once both photos have loaded and the whole hero comparison is on screen below the header, its divider glides from the left edge past its start in 900 ms (from `lg` up to the slider's `widePeak` if set, otherwise 15 points past), easing out, then settles back on it in 500 ms (skipped under reduced motion, stopped by a drag). The hero photos are another (owner's call): as the hero scrolls up past the header, both photos slide down by up to 50% of its height, so they scroll at 50% of the page's speed and the comparison stays aligned. It is CSS only (`src/components/compare-slider/parallax.css`, the slider's `hasParallax`), off under reduced motion and in browsers without scroll-driven animations. The clients band (`src/blocks/clients-band`) is one more exception (owner's call): its cleared clients scroll left in a loop, 3 s per client, linear. It pauses on hover, on focus and with its pause button (WCAG 2.2.2), and under reduced motion it stays still and scrolls by hand.

The 5-step track has no animation; its steps are always visible (owner's call).

The questions' plus turns 45° in 150 ms when one opens.

Never: bounce, overshoot, shake, pulse, parallax (except the hero photos above), scroll fly-ins, staggered card lists, count-up numbers, carousels, shimmer, autoplay (except the clients band above). `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected globally in `src/styles/defaults.css`.
